# Run report 2026-11-01

## Plan

The base held 643 records (about 270 full text, 370 abstract), 14 topic articles, two summaries and the
analyses. The 2026-10-31 maintainer review said that coverage of what PubMed and Europe PMC hold is
complete and pointed to source families not yet searched: J-STAGE, SciELO, DOAJ, ESPE abstracts,
theses, regional and non-English journals, and a list of open-access leads outside PubMed. This run:

1. checked PubMed and Europe PMC for anything new;
2. worked the maintainer's open-access leads;
3. searched the unsearched families (J-STAGE, ESPE, OpenAlex in German, Spanish, Portuguese,
   French; DOAJ; theses; five congress supplements);
4. read every reachable text in full, wrote records, and tried to upgrade abstract records;
5. updated the topics, summaries, indexes, analyses and site.

## What was found and done

- **PubMed and Europe PMC.** The core query now returns 518 hits. The 44 not in the ledger were
  juxtaglomerular cell tumours, which share the JGCT acronym; they were skipped. One new item was
  recorded from its abstract: a Chinese series of 40 granulosa cell tumours including 6 juvenile
  (pmid-23898526). Europe PMC added nothing since 2026-09-01. All 455 PMID items still `found` had
  their PubMed abstracts checked for JGCT terms: none mention JGCT. They stay `found` with a note.
- **Maintainer's open-access leads.** Six records from regional journals (a fatal adult case from
  Japan in 1992, a Brazilian toddler first treated for candidiasis, a Welsh torsion letter, a Turkish
  adult case with a doubtful classification, a Turkish infant testis, a Spanish prenatal testis).
  Most other leads are open-access Elsevier case journals; Elsevier still blocks scripts.
- **J-STAGE** (first systematic search): 13 new Japanese items; 10 full-text records and 1 abstract
  record, including a 1990 fatal bilateral stage IV case in a 3-year-old and a 2026 case of a
  FOXL2-negative granulosa cell tumour at 12 and again at 40.
- **ESPE abstracts site**: one new JGCT abstract (neonatal McCune-Albright syndrome, ruptured JGCT
  with GNAS R201H at 4 months). Two others were already records.
- **Congress supplements**: ESPR 2017 gave one JGCT poster (Ollier disease). ECP 1999, ECP 2003,
  Dublin 2015 and Ochsner 2013 gave no matching text; Turkish 2018 only a passing mention.
- **OpenAlex non-English sweep**: about 75 JGCT items absent from the ledger.
  - 11 German meeting abstracts in Geburtshilfe und Frauenheilkunde and related journals. Thieme
    serves an anti-bot page to scripts, so these rest on the OpenAlex copy of the abstract and are
    marked abstract-only. Two report the same fetal patient and are linked.
  - Spanish, Portuguese, French, Turkish, Russian, Korean, Romanian, Ukrainian and Hungarian
    articles, Moroccan medical theses (Toubkal), French theses. 24 full-text records and 13
    abstract records.
- **Differential diagnosis** (as the maintainer review suggested): two SCCOHT papers, one tumour
  twice reported as high-grade JGCT; a 37-case Sertoli-Leydig series with one case reclassified as
  JGCT; a paediatric Sertoli-Leydig tumour first read as JGCT.
- **Upgrades from abstract to full text**: pmid-27042471 (anaplastic JGCT, stage III, BEP),
  pmid-27993794 (GCIG rare-tumour consensus), pmid-40269311 (single-cell study with a juvenile
  tumour), and the Hungarian testicular review, which supersedes abstract record pmid-42143686.
  Two records carried PMCIDs of other articles (pmid-27042471, pmid-19819941); corrected.

## Tools

- `ledger.py list --origin` filter.
- `epmc_supplement_grep.py` creates its cache directory (it failed on a fresh runner).
- `mkrecord.write` refuses to replace a record written on an earlier day unless `overwrite=True`.
  This run overwrote one existing ESPE record before the guard existed; it was restored from git.
- `check_meta.py` checks PMCIDs against PubMed. `--fix` no longer rewrites titles that differ only
  by a trailing period; it had touched 172 records, which were restored.
- `analyze.py` analyses only full-text patient rows, as the protocol says; abstract rows (new this
  run) stay in the dataset unanalysed.
- `state/cache/` added to `.gitignore`.

## Conflicts and oddities noted (in record notes)

- **McCune-Albright syndrome.** The ESPE 2018 abstract (url-08fbb6ac79f2) states that JGCT had not
  been described in MAS. The base already holds a girl with both diseases (pmid-34286167).
- **Doubtful "juvenile" labels.** Several adult cases are called juvenile with little support:
  - an inhibin- and calretinin-negative tumour (doi-10.5505_aot.2012.33042);
  - a 35-year-old with a virilising tumour (doi-10.1515_sjecr-2017-0016);
  - patients aged 18 to 31 in a Moroccan series (doi-10.21474_ijar01_14578);
  - a 36-year-old recurrence (doi-10.4321_s0378-48352006000700004).
- **Title against text.** doi-10.1055_s-2006-944510 claims a combined juvenile and adult tumour in
  its title, but its abstract diagnoses adult type only.
- **Internal inconsistencies** in several regional case reports: side of the tumour, units and
  mean ages. Each is noted on its record.
