# Run report 2026-10-30 (third run of the day)

## Plan

The previous run (merged as PR #12) left the base full-text only: 232 records, 235
`needs_full_text` items, and about 1,466 `found` items, most with no open-access copy. This
run set out to:

1. find new full-text routes for the blocked items;
2. read whatever those routes opened;
3. triage the untriaged `found` items;
4. check for new literature;
5. update the README, the indexes and the source index, then open a PR.

## What was done

- **New full-text route: the PMC website.** The PMC website serves the article body to a plain
  browser user agent. It answered the old custom user agent with reCAPTCHA. Europe PMC fullTextXML and
  NCBI efetch give no body for PMC articles outside the OA subset: free-to-read articles, NIH author
  manuscripts and the Wiley congress supplements. `tools/fulltext.py` now falls back to
  `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC<id>/`.
  - PMC still sends a reCAPTCHA page when requests come in bursts. The tool backs off for 30, 90
    and 180 s and never tries to solve the captcha.
  - Elsevier/ScienceDirect also answers with a captcha. It was not circumvented.
- **Other routes tried.** OpenAlex `locations` and Semantic Scholar `openAccessPdf` were queried for
  all 200 needs_full_text DOIs, and 43 had an alternative URL.
  - That opened JOSAM, Zenodo, Iranian journal and KoreaScience PDFs, plus several PMC articles
    that Europe PMC had not mapped.
  - Most alternatives were publisher PDFs that block scripts.
  - OJS journals serve full text at `article/download/<id>/<galley>`.
- **Wrong-article guard.** PMC maps PMID 26894074 (a JGCT case) to PMC4740602, which holds an
  unrelated tuberculosis paper. JCDR article pages return only site chrome.
  - `fulltext.py get` now refuses text whose words do not match the expected title.
  - An audit of every record with cached text (31) found no mismatch.
- **Fetch queues.** Over the run, full text was attempted for about 1,260 ledger items:
  - 357 PMC and alternative-URL candidates;
  - 390 untriaged core items, with JGCT in title or abstract from PubMed or OpenAlex;
  - 517 found items with sex cord-stromal, granulosa, FOXL2 or DICER1 keywords in the title;
  - 199 OA items with 1-2 mentions, re-fetched because the runner cache is wiped.

  Paywalled items rarely yielded text: 6 of 390 core items and 3 of 517 keyword items.
- **Ledger triage.**
  - **To `needs_full_text`:** 359 core items with no reachable full text, with the routes
    tried in the note. Another 37 found items whose titles show JGCT relevance were moved after a
    title screen: JGCT in the title, FOXL2/IHC series that tested JGCT, testicular sex cord-stromal
    tumor series, and pediatric granulosa cell tumor cases published before 1984.
  - **Screened by mention context:** about 200 OA texts with 1 or 2 JGCT mentions. Each mention was
    read in context, and 190 were set `skipped` as passing mentions. The others were read in full.
  - **Title screen of the 840 remaining `found` items.** A Sonnet sub-agent classified the titles and the
    coordinator checked samples: 240 became `needs_full_text` (likely relevant, no reachable full text;
    15 of them were fetch-tried first), 138 became `skipped` (clearly unrelated, e.g. PCOS), and the rest
    stay `found` as unsure.
  - **Noted but left `found`:** about 540 items with no OA copy and no sign of JGCT relevance,
    each with a note saying so.
- **Reading.**
  - 5 Sonnet sub-agents read 56 full texts.
  - The coordinator read 6 conference abstracts in 4 SIOP/ASPHO supplements, one Persian article and
    one Turkish case report.
  - 39 new records were written. Of the full texts read, 22 were skipped and 9 turned out not to
    be the article.
- **New discovery.** PubMed and Europe PMC JGCT queries found nothing new: still 462 PubMed hits,
  all in the ledger. A pediatric granulosa query for 2024-2026 added 2 items.

## Conflicts and oddities noted (in record notes)

- **Karachi (pmid-24606464).** Its table lists virilization as the most common JGCT presentation,
  and 2 of 15 recurred, one as a skin metastasis.
- **TGM95 (url-039c1963d955).** It supports chemotherapy after rupture: 0/11 relapsed with VIP, 4/4
  relapsed without it. Histologies are mixed, and it sits against the IC2 doubts of pmid-41367860
  and pmid-40350706. Already covered in the README's IC2 paragraph, so no new `conflicts_with`
  link was added.
- **Unsupported "juvenile" labels** in adult-age records: pmid-30123871, pmid-18205951, and
  pmid-40037278, whose text calls the same tumor both JGCT and germ cell tumor.
- **HIPEC review (pmid-30911605).** The abstract says 1 JGCT; the body says 2.
