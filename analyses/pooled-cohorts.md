# Pooled descriptive tables from the cohort studies

**What this is.** Study-level counts copied from the records of the larger JGCT series and registries, shown side by side, with a crude pooled proportion and a random-effects pooled proportion (DerSimonian-Laird on the logit scale) where at least three non-overlapping cohorts report the same thing. It is the maintenance agent's own analysis, unreviewed, and not a source. Rows marked † rest on abstracts only. Overlapping cohorts (the same registry reported more than once, the same hospital at two dates, pooled reviews of published cases) are shown but only the row marked primary enters a pooled figure. See [the protocol](protocol.md).

## Mitotic index and relapse

| cohort | events, fewer than 20 per 10 HPF | events, 20 or more | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| STEP/MAKEI 2025 (89 ovarian JGCT, 2001-2019) | 0/61 | 4/28 | all 89 ovarian JGCT; threshold 20 per 10 HPF |
| OTST Registry 2025 (70 ovarian JGCT, 2001-2024) | 0/16 | 5/18 | low-stage patients treated with surgery alone |

Only two cohorts give counts, and they overlap neither each other nor the Kiel registry figure (event-free survival 1.0 below 20 versus 0.48 at 20 or more, counts not stated †). Two cohorts are not pooled. Together they are the strongest consistent signal in the JGCT literature: every relapse with a known index was in the high group.

## Chemotherapy at stage IC: what each source reports, not pooled

| source | patients | with chemotherapy | without chemotherapy | what it shows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| STEP 2025 (full text) | 18 at IC2/IC3 | 13, no relapse | 5, no relapse | no relapses either way; IC1 (14): 1 treated, none relapsed |
| OTST 2025 (full text) | IC2 to IC3 subgroup | most | few | 3-year EFS 63.6%; effect not analysable, sicker patients treated |
| Stage IC meta-analysis 2025 (full text) | 33 JGCT of 695 | 17: 4 relapsed | 16: 0 relapsed | no benefit; authors attribute to confounding by indication |
| TGM-95 (abstract and SIOP abstract) | 15 with rupture or malignant ascites, mixed histology | 11 VIP: 0 relapsed | 4: 4 relapsed, 2 died (both Sertoli-Leydig) | only series favouring chemotherapy; mixed histologies |
| MAKEI 2003 † | 21 at IC | 9 treated | 12 observed | IC event-free survival 0.76 overall; recommends chemotherapy for preoperative rupture or malignant ascites |
| Plantaz 1992 † | 16 stage III (Wollner), 1965-1990 | 13 chemotherapy overall |  | no evidence that rupture required chemotherapy |
| Wang 2022 † | 26 IC of 35 | 31 of 35 treated | 4 | adjuvant chemotherapy not associated with disease-free survival |
| Published cases here | see published-cases.md |  |  | counts only |

These cannot be pooled: three different definitions of stage IC, two of chemotherapy indication, non-random treatment assignment in every one, and in the only series where untreated patients relapsed the histologies were mixed. The honest summary is that no study has shown chemotherapy to change relapse at stage IC in JGCT, and no study was designed to.
