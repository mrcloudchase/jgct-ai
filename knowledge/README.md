# Knowledge base

Everything here comes from sources read in full. Every factual sentence cites the records it rests on.

- [For clinicians and researchers](summary/clinicians.md) and [For parents and patients](summary/families.md): the two summaries of what the base says.
- [Topics](topics/): one synthesized, cited article per subject, kept current as records change.
- [Records](records/): one file per source, with what it says and where its full text was read. [Index](INDEX.md).
- [Schema](schema/record.schema.json): what every record must contain.

Not medical advice. Every record is a draft until a human has checked it against its source.
