# For clinicians and researchers

This is a synthesis of the sources recorded in this knowledge base, not medical advice, and every record is a `draft` until a human reviews it. It reports what sources say and where they disagree. It recommends nothing for any individual patient. Each section below is a short account with a link to the full topic article, where every relevant source is laid out.

### The gist

- JGCT is a rare sex cord-stromal tumor, about 5% of granulosa cell tumors but the most common ovarian sex cord-stromal tumor of childhood; unlike adult-type tumors it lacks the FOXL2 C134W mutation [pmid-38136408, pmid-23641362, pmid-22525408].
- Ovarian JGCT presents at a median age of about 10 years and also in adolescents and adults. Testicular JGCT is a tumor of infants (median age 16 days in STEP), has never been reported to metastasize, and a 2025 pathology consensus proposes renaming it "benign juvenile granulosa cell tumour" [pmid-40272823, pmid-32719989, pmid-40528632].
- In prepubertal girls isosexual precocity is the leading sign (27 of 38 under 8 years in STEP); older girls present with abdominal pain or distension, menstrual change or an acute abdomen. Inhibin B was elevated in 38 of 42 OTST patients at diagnosis [pmid-40272823, pmid-40267023].
- About four in five tumors are FIGO stage I at diagnosis: 81% in OTST, 84 of 89 in STEP [pmid-40267023, pmid-40272823].
- The 2026 European recommendations: unilateral oophorectomy by an approach that avoids rupture, with peritoneal cytology and biopsy of suspicious areas and no routine lymphadenectomy; surveillance for stage IA, IB and IC1; 3 to 4 cycles of cisplatin-based chemotherapy (PEI/VIP or BEP) for IC2/IC3; at least 4 to 6 cycles for stage II-III; re-excision with oophorectomy after ovary-sparing surgery when the mitotic count is high [pmid-42504512].
- Whether chemotherapy helps at stage IC is unsettled: a 2025 meta-analysis found no benefit in its 33 JGCT patients, while STEP saw no relapses at IC2/IC3 with chemotherapy given to 13 of 18 [pmid-40350706, pmid-40272823].
- Outcomes for stage I are excellent: 5-year event-free survival 0.95 and overall survival 0.97 for ovarian JGCT in STEP; 3-year event-free survival 73% and overall survival 92% across all stages in OTST, worse from stage IC2 upward [pmid-40272823, pmid-40267023].
- Mitotic index is the strongest histological prognostic factor: every OTST recurrence with a known index had more than 19 mitoses per 10 high-power fields, and all four STEP relapses had high mitotic rates, three of them in stage IA tumors [pmid-40267023, pmid-40272823].
- Relapse comes early (13 of 14 within 2 years in OTST) and carries a poor prognosis (long-term survival about 20% or lower per the European recommendations). Follow-up is examination, tumor markers if raised at diagnosis, and ultrasound every 3 months for the first 2 years [pmid-40267023, pmid-42504512].
- Somatic AKT1 pleckstrin-homology-domain duplications (10 of 16 in the index study) and GNAS R201 mutations (about 30% in reviews) are the known drivers; DICER1 variants are uncommon and testing is advised for anaplastic tumors. Small cell carcinoma of the ovary, hypercalcemic type, is the key differential and is separated by SMARCA4 immunohistochemistry [pmid-26137586, pmid-38136408, pmid-42504512, pmid-31844183].
- Associations: Ollier disease and Maffucci syndrome (a shared somatic IDH1 mutation in one case), McCune-Albright syndrome (shared mosaic GNAS R201C), DICER1 syndrome rarely, and biallelic germline MAD2L1BP in one sibling pair [pmid-37324278, pmid-34286167, pmid-42504512, pmid-37796616].
- No JGCT-specific trial exists, and several of the field's classic series and guidelines remain unread here because their full text is paywalled (see Gaps below).

### What JGCT is

A sex cord-stromal tumor of the ovary or testis, about 5% of granulosa cell tumors yet the most common ovarian sex cord-stromal tumor of childhood, and about 70 to 75% of pediatric ovarian sex cord-stromal tumors [pmid-38136408, pmid-22525408, pmid-32355841, pmid-40723200]. It is distinct from adult-type granulosa cell tumor: the FOXL2 C134W mutation that defines the adult type is rare or absent, relapse comes within about 2 years rather than after decades, and the drivers are AKT1 and GNAS [pmid-23641362, pmid-38136408, pmid-42504512, pmid-26137586]. Ovarian and testicular JGCT behave so differently that recent sources treat them as separate entities [pmid-40272823, pmid-42421431]. Full account: [What JGCT is](../topics/what-jgct-is.md).

### Who gets it and how it presents

The two registries define the modern picture: STEP, 89 ovarian JGCT with median age 9.8 years and 44% under 8 [pmid-40272823]; OTST, 70 ovarian JGCT with median age 10 years, 93% under 20 [pmid-40267023]. Presentation is abdominal pain (54%) and distension (45%), precocious puberty (26%) and virilization (12%) in OTST; 27 of 38 girls under 8 in STEP had isosexual precocity and 6% presented with torsion [pmid-40267023, pmid-40272823]. Less common presentations recorded in this base include androgen-driven virilization, massive ascites and pseudo-Meigs syndrome, secondary amenorrhea, paraneoplastic hypercalcemia, interleukin-6-driven fever and delayed puberty, and rupture in pregnancy [pmid-36112066, pmid-23424695, pmid-36647328, pmid-24371683, pmid-31582893, pmid-37426562]. Testicular JGCT is a tumor of infancy: median age 16 days in STEP, no hormone signs, no relapse [pmid-40272823]. Full accounts: [Signs and symptoms](../topics/signs-and-symptoms.md), [Testicular JGCT](../topics/testicular-jgct.md), [JGCT in adults and in pregnancy](../topics/jgct-in-adults-and-pregnancy.md).

### Diagnosis: markers and imaging

Inhibin B is the most consistent serum marker (38 of 42 raised in OTST; inhibin A 22 of 34; CA-125 20 of 42; testosterone 9 of 21; AFP 2 of 46) and falls to normal after complete resection [pmid-40267023, pmid-34487069]. Among 1,128 pediatric and young-adult ovarian masses, inhibin B predicted malignancy with 70% sensitivity and 85% specificity [pmid-41000187]. Inhibin B is usable in pregnancy because pregnancy raises inhibin A but not B [pmid-41797949]. Imaging shows a large, usually unilateral, solid-cystic mass (median 12 cm) that cannot be told from other ovarian tumors; the testicular tumor is a multiseptated "Swiss cheese" mass [pmid-40267023, pmid-26054417, pmid-33209713]. In infants AFP is physiologically high and cannot exclude yolk sac tumor until about 6 months [pmid-16985566, pmid-33201299]. Full account: [Diagnosis: markers and imaging](../topics/diagnosis-markers-and-imaging.md).

### Pathology and differential diagnosis

Follicles of varying size with mucinous fluid, rounded nuclei without grooves, luminal and theca-like components, frequent mitoses; positive for inhibin, calretinin, SF-1 and FOXL2 protein; SMARCA4 retained [pmid-36748172, pmid-39773640, pmid-26356327]. In OTST 37 of 60 tumors had a mitotic index above 19 per 10 high-power fields [pmid-40267023]. The differentials that matter: small cell carcinoma of the ovary, hypercalcemic type (misdiagnosed as JGCT in 6 of 16 in one series; SMARCA4 loss separates it, and COV434, the old "JGCT" cell line, is now classed as SCCOHT) [pmid-31844183, pmid-33328126]; Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor and gynandroblastoma, including DICER1-mutant tumors reclassified on central review [pmid-29037807, pmid-37248399]; adult-type tumor with mixed morphology [pmid-34688077]; and sclerosing peritonitis mimicking peritoneal spread [pmid-24783102]. The European recommendations require reference pathology [pmid-42504512]. Full account: [Pathology and differential diagnosis](../topics/pathology-and-differential-diagnosis.md).

### Molecular biology and inherited risk

Somatic AKT1 pleckstrin-homology-domain in-frame duplications were found in 10 of 16 JGCT, with a putative AKT1 driver in 14 of 16 [pmid-26137586]; GNAS R201 mutations are reported in about 30% in reviews, and TERT promoter, TP53 and rare DICER1 RNase IIIb variants in small subsets [pmid-38136408, pmid-40267023, pmid-39773640]. Testicular tumors lack AKT1 and GNAS alterations and show monosomy 10 [pmid-42421431]. Germline and mosaic associations: Ollier disease and Maffucci syndrome (shared somatic IDH1 R132C in tumor and enchondroma; at least 12 to 15 reported cases) [pmid-37324278, pmid-39601780, pmid-32030011]; McCune-Albright syndrome (shared mosaic GNAS R201C) [pmid-34286167]; DICER1 syndrome, rarely, with testing advised for anaplastic or high-mitotic tumors [url-1b49311e69d4, pmid-39400264]; biallelic germline MAD2L1BP in siblings [pmid-37796616]; single reports with xeroderma pigmentosum C, neurofibromatosis 1 and Donohue syndrome [pmid-37567969, pmid-35848651, pmid-25153212]. Full accounts: [Genetics of JGCT](../topics/genetics-of-jgct.md), [Syndromes and inherited risk](../topics/syndromes-and-inherited-risk.md).

### Staging and surgery

FIGO staging applies; about four in five tumors are stage I [pmid-40267023, pmid-40272823]. The 2026 European recommendations (EXPeRT/ERN PaedCan, level III): open unilateral salpingo-oophorectomy avoiding rupture, peritoneal cytology, inspection and biopsy of suspicious areas, no routine lymphadenectomy (no nodal metastases are reported in JGCT series), and completion oophorectomy after ovary-sparing surgery when the mitotic count is high [pmid-42504512, pmid-35849655]. Intra-operative rupture upstages to IC1 and is common with large cystic tumors; in STEP 36 of 89 were IC [pmid-40272823]. Fertility-sparing surgery is the norm; pregnancy followed in 42% of JGCT patients in one review [pmid-39349505, pmid-35437273]. Full account: [Staging and surgery](../topics/staging-and-surgery.md).

### Chemotherapy and the stage IC question

The European recommendations give surveillance to IA, IB and IC1, 3 to 4 cycles of PEI/VIP or BEP to IC2 and IC3, and at least 4 to 6 cycles to stage II and III [pmid-42504512]. Five guidelines agree that completely resected IA to IC1 may avoid chemotherapy and that IC2 to IC3 should have it, and differ on IC1 and debulking [pmid-36980387]. The evidence is thin and conflicting: a 2025 stage IC meta-analysis found no recurrence benefit in its 33 JGCT patients [pmid-40350706]; a review of 94 published pediatric cases found 3 of 5 untreated IC2 patients alive and questioned chemotherapy [pmid-41367860]; STEP saw no relapses at IC2/IC3 with chemotherapy in 13 of 18, while OTST reported 3-year EFS of 63.6% at IC2 to IC3 [pmid-40272823, pmid-40267023]; in TGM95, 4 of 4 children with rupture or malignant ascites who went without chemotherapy relapsed, against 0 of 11 given VIP, in a mixed-histology group [url-039c1963d955]. Full account: [Chemotherapy and the stage IC question](../topics/chemotherapy-and-the-stage-ic-question.md).

### Outcomes and prognostic factors

STEP: 5-year EFS 0.95, OS 0.97 for ovarian JGCT; OS by stage IA 98%, IC1 100%, IC2/IC3 100%, II/III 80%; EFS 61 of 61 with mitotic rate under 20 versus 24 of 28 at 20 or more [pmid-40272823]. OTST: 3-year EFS 73% and OS 92%; EFS by stage IA 80.2%, IC1 87.4%, IC2 to IC3 63.6%, II to IV 48.0%; EFS 55.5% with a high mitotic index versus 100% with a low one, and every relapse with a known index was high [pmid-40267023]. Smaller series: no deaths at IA/IB and 3 of 11 at IC or above in the 2017 OTST report; 2 of 13 deaths in South Africa; 2 of 15 recurrences in Karachi; none in 11 Korean patients [pmid-29037807, pmid-33560080, pmid-24606464, pmid-21998762]. Full account: [Prognosis and the mitotic index](../topics/prognosis-and-mitotic-index.md).

### Relapse and salvage

Relapse is early (OTST median 8.5 months, 13 of 14 within 2 years), mostly peritoneal, and often fatal: 8 of 14 OTST relapses died; long-term survival after relapse is about 20% or lower; median OS after recurrence was 32 months for pure JGCT versus 182 months for adult-type tumors in one registry [pmid-40267023, pmid-42504512, pmid-40815979]. No harmonized salvage strategy exists. Recorded approaches: resection plus platinum (PEI, BEP), carboplatin-paclitaxel, bevacizumab, radiotherapy, high-dose chemotherapy with stem-cell rescue, cytoreduction with HIPEC (2 JGCT patients in the literature), and GnRH blockade as an add-on [pmid-40272823, pmid-41367860, pmid-39773640, pmid-30911605, pmid-26500075]. AKT1 is the natural target but no AKT-directed treatment is reported [pmid-26137586, pmid-40267023]. Full account: [Relapse and salvage treatment](../topics/relapse-and-salvage.md).

### Follow-up and life after treatment

Examination with abdominal and pelvic ultrasound every 3 months for 2 years, then at longer intervals; tumor markers only if raised at diagnosis; no routine imaging above the diaphragm [pmid-42504512]. Other sources suggest 3 to 5 years [pmid-41367860, pmid-40267023]. Inhibin B relapses are not always marker-positive (3 of 5 in OTST) [pmid-40267023]. Endocrine signs regress after surgery; central precocious puberty from priming occurred in 2 of 57 prepubertal cases and needs to be told from relapse [pmid-35371651, pmid-41367860]. Metachronous contralateral tumors occur in about 4% at a median 2.3 years [pmid-41367860, pmid-36748172]. Testicular JGCT needs only annual clinical and ultrasound review [pmid-33201299]. Full account: [Follow-up and life after treatment](../topics/follow-up-and-life-after-treatment.md).

### Where the literature disagrees

- **Chemotherapy at stage IC2** (above) [pmid-42504512, pmid-41367860, pmid-40350706, pmid-40272823, pmid-40267023, url-039c1963d955].
- **DICER1 in JGCT.** 0 of 19 germline and 0 of 2 tumor mutations in OTST [pmid-29037807]; 1 of 14 hotspot mutations [pmid-22187960]; 0 of 4 pure JGCT [pmid-24136150]; "6 to 23%" in one review [pmid-38136408].
- **FOXL2 in JGCT.** 1 of 10 in one review [pmid-23641362]; absent in another [pmid-38136408].
- **Drivers.** A case report states that no driver is known [pmid-39877787]; sequencing series find AKT1 and other alterations [pmid-39773640, pmid-26137586].
- **COV434.** Used as a JGCT model [pmid-31311113]; now classed as SCCOHT [pmid-33328126].
- **Karyotyping boys with testicular JGCT.** Advised for all in older reviews [pmid-16985566]; only with genital anomalies in recent German guidance [pmid-33201299].

The site's "Where sources disagree" page lists every recorded disagreement, built from the `disagreements` field of each record. Pooled reviews re-count published cases, so counts across `overlaps_with` records must not be added together.
