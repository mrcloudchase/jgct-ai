# Chemotherapy: who needs it, and the stage IC question

Most children with JGCT need no chemotherapy. When it is given, it is a platinum-based regimen borrowed from germ cell tumor treatment, four cycles, sometimes six. The hard cases are the stage IC tumors, where a burst capsule or tumor cells in the abdominal fluid raise the risk of relapse but the evidence that chemotherapy lowers it is thin.

## What the guidelines say

The 2026 European Standard Clinical Practice recommendations (EXPeRT/ERN PaedCan): stage IA and IB, surveillance; IC1 (spill during surgery), surveillance, with chemotherapy considered if the mitotic count is high; IC2 and IC3, 3 to 4 cycles of cisplatin, etoposide and ifosfamide (PEI/VIP) or bleomycin, etoposide and cisplatin (BEP); stage II-III, at least 4 to 6 cycles; 5 to 6 cycles may be considered for high mitotic index [pmid-42504512]. A 2023 comparison of five guidelines found agreement that completely resected IA to IC1 can avoid chemotherapy and IC2/IC3 needs it, with disagreement on IC1 and on debulking [pmid-36980387]. The NCI PDQ summary gives the same picture [url-3b3cdea64edb]. Earlier COG/MAKEI guidance recommended 4 or 6 cycles of PEB for IC with preoperative rupture or malignant ascites and for II-IV, and observation for intraoperative rupture with a low mitotic count [pmid-22525408]. The 2021 EXPeRT/PARTNER consensus, known here from its abstract, recommends cisplatin-based chemotherapy when spillage or metastases worsen the outlook and stratified adjuvant therapy for unfavorable risk profiles [pmid-33760357]; the 2020 ESGO-SIOPE guideline for ages 15 to 25 is also known from its abstract only [pmid-32615119]. A French pediatric review calls cisplatin chemotherapy mandatory after rupture or peritoneal dissemination (abstract only) [pmid-26028491].

## The evidence behind it

- **STEP (89 ovarian JGCT).** Chemotherapy went to 2 of 52 stage IA, 1 of 14 IC1, 13 of 18 IC2/IC3 and all 5 stage II/III patients; no IC1 or IC2/IC3 patient relapsed; 5-year event-free survival was 0.95. In the historical MAKEI cohort, where IC2 patients mostly went untreated, 5-year survival at IC2 was 50%. The authors conclude that restricting chemotherapy to IC2 and above worked [pmid-40272823].
- **OTST (70 patients).** 30% received chemotherapy, 17 of 21 with BEP; 3-year event-free survival was 87% at IC1 and 64% at IC2-IC3, but the effect of chemotherapy could not be analyzed because sicker patients got it [pmid-40267023].
- **TGM-95 (France).** Of 15 children with rupture or malignant ascites, 11 given VIP had no recurrence and all 4 untreated relapsed within 18 months; histologies were mixed and both deaths were Sertoli-Leydig tumors [url-039c1963d955]. The full paper's abstract adds that 23 of its 38 ovarian tumors were JGCT, that 2 of 23 completely resected tumors without adjuvant treatment relapsed, and 5-year event-free and overall survival of 85% and 94% [pmid-26206391].
- **The German lineage, from abstracts only.** In the first prospective cohort (33 JGCT, 1997), 9 children received cisplatin-based chemotherapy and event-free survival was 0.75 at 168 months [pmid-9190974]. The 2003 MAKEI analysis of 54 ovarian stromal tumors gave event-free survival of 1.0 at IA, 0.76 at IC and 0.67 at II-III, and recommended chemotherapy at IC only with preoperative rupture or malignant ascites, especially with high mitotic activity [pmid-12805338]; all 7 MAKEI patients with stage II or higher JGCT reached complete remission on 4 or 6 cycles of cisplatin-based therapy, and 6 of 7 were alive at a median 47 months [pmid-12165898].
- **Older French series.** The 40-patient multicenter review of 1965 to 1990 found no evidence that rupture with hemoperitoneum required chemotherapy and no survival gain from chemotherapy in the rare malignant forms (abstract only) [pmid-1300967].
- **Recent single-center series, abstracts only.** Adjuvant chemotherapy was not associated with disease-free survival in 35 early-stage patients, 31 of whom received it [pmid-34902562]; 24 of 30 Monza patients had surveillance only and the 3 deaths were all advanced-stage [pmid-39561630]; 2 of 17 stage I MITO-9 patients received chemotherapy and none of the 17 relapsed [pmid-33583630]; 12 of 19 Wuhan patients and 6 of 18 Fudan stage I patients received it, with one relapse in each series [pmid-33904645, pmid-42706059]; TGM13 gave chemotherapy to 15 of 63 ovarian stromal tumors with 3-year progression-free survival of 89% [pmid-38259036].
- **Meta-analysis, stage IC granulosa cell tumors (2025).** Across 695 adult and juvenile patients, chemotherapy did not reduce recurrence or death; in the 33 JGCT patients, 4 of 17 treated relapsed and 0 of 16 observed did, a non-significant difference that the authors say reflects sicker patients being treated [pmid-40350706].
- **94 published pediatric cases.** 3 of 5 IC2 patients had no chemotherapy and were alive at 3 to 8 years [pmid-41367860].

## Regimens

PEI was used in all but one STEP patient (cisplatin 100 mg/m2, etoposide 300 mg/m2, ifosfamide 7,500 mg/m2 per cycle, 3 to 6 cycles) [pmid-40272823]; BEP in 17 of 21 OTST patients [pmid-40267023]. In adults with sex cord-stromal tumors, carboplatin and paclitaxel performed like BEP in a randomized phase II trial that enrolled no JGCT and closed early [url-68575db179a7], and the European recommendations note that result while keeping cisplatin regimens for children [pmid-42504512]. Bleomycin carries lung toxicity and ifosfamide gonadal toxicity, considerations the 2025 management review raises when choosing between PEB and PEI [pmid-41367860, pmid-40350706]. The 1996 phase II trial that established BEP for poor-prognosis sex cord-stromal tumors included one JGCT among nine patients and saw an 83% response rate but only one durable remission in seven metastatic patients (abstract only) [pmid-8602303]; the Gynecologic Cancer InterGroup consensus asks for alternatives to BEP and validated prognostic markers (abstract only) [pmid-27993794]. Two Armenian adolescents with stage IC disease were given 4 cycles of PEI on the advice of the EXPeRT virtual consultation board (abstract only) [pmid-34674583].

## Where sources disagree

ESMO advises chemotherapy for all stage IC JGCT; ESGO-SIOPE and the European pediatric recommendations spare IC1 [pmid-40350706, pmid-36980387, pmid-42504512]. STEP saw no IC2/IC3 relapses with chemotherapy [pmid-40272823] while OTST's IC2-IC3 group fared worst among stage I despite most receiving it [pmid-40267023]. The meta-analysis found no benefit at all, but its JGCT numbers are tiny [pmid-40350706].

## What is unknown

Whether a stage IA tumor with a very high mitotic index should get chemotherapy. Both 2025 registry papers raise it: OTST saw five low-stage, high-index, surgery-only recurrences and three deaths [pmid-40267023]; STEP's three stage IA relapses all had high mitotic counts, and its authors call the question open [pmid-40272823]. No randomized trial exists or is likely, given the numbers [pmid-40272823].
