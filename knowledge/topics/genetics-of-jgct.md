# Genetics of JGCT

Almost all JGCTs are sporadic: the gene changes are in the tumor, not inherited. The two best-established drivers both switch on growth signalling inside granulosa cells. Knowing them matters for diagnosis, for separating JGCT from look-alikes, and as possible targets for future drugs.

## AKT1

In 16 tumors from girls under 15, 10 (more than 60%) carried in-frame tandem duplications in exon 3 of AKT1, in the pleckstrin-homology domain; the duplicated proteins sat at the cell membrane, were strongly phosphorylated and were active even without serum, and 14 of 16 tumors had some putative AKT1 driver change [pmid-26137586]. A 2025 Czech series found AKT1 duplications in 2 of 5 tumors and loss of PTEN protein in the 3 AKT1-wild-type tumors, so the authors propose that most JGCTs activate the PI3K/AKT/mTOR pathway one way or another [pmid-39773640]. OTST sequencing found AKT1 duplications in 3 patients [pmid-40267023], and a 7-year-old's tumor in cBioPortal carries an AKT1 insertion [url-6657783a29d4]. In flies, the mutant human AKT1 enlarges cells and disturbs tissue polarity [pmid-41237900]. Reviews quote AKT1 alterations in 29 to 60% of JGCT [pmid-38136408, pmid-27813081, pmid-40930934]. Transcriptomes of four AKT1-mutant tumors showed only private variants, pointing to the duplication as the driver where GNAS is normal, and AKT inhibitors modulated the mutant proteins in vitro (abstract only) [pmid-26362254]. The largest molecular study, 31 tumors with a 580-gene panel, RNA sequencing and methylation arrays, is known here from its abstract: frequent KMT2C-truncating and other SWI/SNF-complex mutations, the known AKT1 and DICER1 hotspots, TERT rearrangements with CLPTM1L or DROSHA in 13%, FGFR1 up-regulation in the rest, and a methylation profile distinct from adult-type tumors [pmid-35031544].

## GNAS

Activating GNAS R201 mutations (the same mutation that causes McCune-Albright syndrome) are reported in about 30% of JGCT in reviews [pmid-38136408, pmid-32485873]; OTST found GNAS p.R201C in 2 tumors [pmid-40267023], and one 15-year-old had the identical GNAS R201C in her tumor and in her fibrous dysplasia bone, with normal blood, proving mosaicism [pmid-34286167]. The AKT1 discovery cohort had no GNAS mutations [pmid-26137586], so the two drivers may be largely separate groups. The original GNAS study, 30 French children, is known from its abstract: R201C or R201H in 9 (30%), confined to tumor granulosa cells, and 7 of the 9 were stage IC or recurred, so the authors proposed it as an adverse marker [pmid-16507630]; a consultation series found a GNAS variant in 1 of 4 JGCT (abstract only) [pmid-32782216].

## Other recurrent changes

A review of the genomic landscape lists KMT2C (52%), ARID1A (39%), KMT2D (35%), TERT promoter variants (19%) and TERT rearrangements (13%), TP53 (up to 16%), and DNA-repair gene variants [pmid-38136408]. OTST found TERT promoter variants in 2 and TP53 variants in 4 patients [pmid-40267023]; the Czech series found a TERT promoter mutation in one recurrent tumor and an EP400 mutation in another [pmid-39773640].

## FOXL2: the absent marker

FOXL2 C134W defines adult-type granulosa cell tumor and is essentially absent from JGCT: 1 of 10 in the index paper [pmid-19516027, pmid-23641362], 0 of 5 [pmid-39773640], 0 of 1 in a late-recurring case [pmid-32341591], and from abstracts only 0 of 18 [pmid-21623383], 1 of 8 [pmid-26033501], 0 of 2 [pmid-24689977], 0 of 7 [pmid-32561911] and 0 of 47 once two mutant tumors were reclassified as adult type [pmid-32910017]. FOXL2 protein is nevertheless usually expressed [pmid-39773640], though it was absent or reduced in 14 of 26 French childhood tumors and in 2 of 3 tumors in another study (abstracts only) [pmid-17430735, pmid-20693978].

## DICER1: rare, and often a sign the diagnosis is something else

DICER1 RNase IIIb hotspot mutations were found in 1 of 14 JGCT in the NEJM discovery study [pmid-22187960] and 0 of 4 in another [pmid-24136150]; the OTST registry found no germline DICER1 mutation in 19 JGCT patients and none in 2 sequenced tumors [pmid-29037807]; reviews quote 6 to 23% [pmid-38136408]. Two DICER1-mutant tumors called JGCT locally were gynandroblastomas on central review [pmid-29037807], and a JGCT in an xeroderma pigmentosum patient carried DICER1 mutations [pmid-37567969]. The European recommendations and GeneReviews advise DICER1 testing when a JGCT is anaplastic or has a high mitotic index [pmid-42504512, url-1b49311e69d4, pmid-39400264]. From abstracts only: 3 of 47 confirmed JGCT (6%) carried RNase IIIb mutations once a DICER1-mutant gynandroblastoma was excluded [pmid-32910017]; 2 of 8 in a French group study [pmid-26033501]; 0 of 5 by immunohistochemistry [pmid-41839237]; 2 of 10 anaplastic tumors [pmid-41036825].

## Models and mechanisms

Smad1/Smad5 knockout mice develop metastatic tumors resembling JGCT; TGF-beta signalling protects the tumor cells from apoptosis, and a soy diet accelerated tumors in the mice [pmid-27104151, pmid-25243859, pmid-25165122]; human JGCT samples showed active TGF-beta/SMAD2/3 but not beta-catenin signalling (abstract only) [pmid-19819941]. SWR-strain mice develop spontaneous juvenile-type tumors under multigenic, partly imprinted control (loci Gct1 on chromosome 4, Gct4 and Gct6 on X, others), initiated before ovarian maturation and possibly by ovarian androgens (abstracts only) [pmid-9721880, pmid-15735010, pmid-14678975, pmid-8339285]. Results from the COV434 cell line, including tyrosine kinase inhibitor and pollutant studies (abstracts only) [pmid-26067856, pmid-33571556], must be read knowing it is now classed as small cell carcinoma of the ovary, hypercalcemic type [pmid-33328126].

## Chromosomes and older markers

From abstracts only: trisomy 12 was found in 3 of 4 pediatric JGCT, 4 of 7 in another study and 25 to 63% of granulosa cell tumors generally, and monosomy 22 in 40%, with no prognostic value [pmid-1466394, pmid-21552888, pmid-8598334, pmid-12218213, pmid-8697433]. INK4 gene silencing occurred in adult but not juvenile tumors [pmid-12203782]; one of two JGCT expressed EGFR, HER3 and HER4 [pmid-16330088]; p53 staining and the highest Ki67 indices were features of the juvenile type in a 1996 study that nonetheless saw no juvenile recurrences [pmid-8600043]; a single JGCT among 5,573 tumors carried androgen receptor splice variants [pmid-42727615].

## What this means and what is unknown

No targeted drug has been tested in JGCT; AKT inhibitors are the obvious candidates and OTST authors call for tumor sequencing in high-risk or recurrent disease [pmid-40267023, pmid-26137586]. Which combination of changes makes the roughly 10% of tumors that behave malignantly is not known [pmid-40528632]; TP53 inactivation with MYC-family amplification in anaplastic tumors is the first candidate (abstract only) [pmid-41036825]. Testicular JGCT lack AKT1 and GNAS alterations and show monosomy 10 in 8 of 14 (abstract only) [pmid-36813116].
