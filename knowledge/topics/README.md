# Topics

One article per subject, synthesized from the records, for both audiences. Each article says what every relevant source reports, where sources agree, where they disagree, and what remains unknown. Every factual sentence cites record IDs in brackets, like `[pmid-40272823]`. Articles are Markdown files named for their subject, for example `chemotherapy-after-rupture.md`, `testicular-jgct.md`, `akt1.md`, each starting with a level-one heading that is its title and a one-paragraph plain-language opening before the technical detail.

The site renders every article here under Topics. Articles are rewritten whenever the records behind them change.
