# Prognosis and the mitotic index

Most children with JGCT are cured by the operation. The outlook depends on how far the tumor had spread when it was found and, the newest evidence says, on how fast its cells were dividing. Nearly all the trouble, when it comes, comes within two years.

## Survival by stage

- **STEP (89 ovarian JGCT, 2001-2019):** 5-year event-free survival 0.95, overall survival 0.97; survival by stage IA 98%, IC1 100%, IC2/IC3 100%, II/III 80% [pmid-40272823].
- **OTST (70 patients, 2001-2024):** 3-year event-free survival 73% and overall survival 92% across all stages; event-free survival 80% at IA, 87% at IC1, 64% at IC2-IC3, 48% at II-IV; 14 recurrences and 8 deaths [pmid-40267023].
- **Older and smaller series:** 10-year event-free survival 0.88 for 34 JGCT in the German cohort; mitoses under 20 per 10 high-power fields gave 100% 10-year event-free survival versus 53% above [pmid-22525408]. A Korean series of 11 had no recurrence over a mean 70 months [pmid-21998762]; a Turkish series of 10 had none over 58 months [pmid-25788847]; a Chinese cohort of 15 early-stage patients had 3 relapses [doi-10.21037_gpm-22-45]; 2 of 13 South African JGCT patients died, both with stage III-IV disease [pmid-33560080].
- **Pooled published cases:** 95.7% of 47 stage I patients alive, 81% of 16 advanced-stage patients alive at a mean 4.4 years, but a quarter had less than 18 months of follow-up [pmid-41367860].

## The mitotic index

The mitotic index is the count of dividing cells per 10 high-power microscope fields. In OTST it was the strongest predictor: 3-year event-free survival was 55% with more than 19 mitoses and 100% with 19 or fewer; all 13 recurrences with a known index had a high index; and among low-stage patients treated with surgery alone, 0 of 16 with a low index recurred versus 5 of 18 with a high index [pmid-40267023]. In STEP, event-free survival was 61 of 61 with fewer than 20 mitoses versus 24 of 28 above; stage itself was not significantly linked to outcome [pmid-40272823]. The European recommendations rate it prognostic [pmid-42504512]. Both registries recommend that every JGCT report state the mitotic index and that chemotherapy be considered individually for low-stage, high-index tumors [pmid-40267023, pmid-40272823].

## Other risk factors

Larger tumors trended toward recurrence in OTST (hazard ratio 1.13 per cm; over 12 cm, 4.5) [pmid-40267023]. Preoperative rupture and malignant ascites were historically the strongest adverse features, with 5-year survival of 50% at IC2 in the old German cohort [pmid-40272823, pmid-22525408]. Incomplete or organ-sparing surgery preceded three of four STEP relapses [pmid-40272823]. Marked nuclear pleomorphism alone was not adverse in the German analysis [pmid-22525408].

## Timing of relapse

Recurrence is early: median 8.5 months and 13 of 14 within 2 years in OTST [pmid-40267023]; 5.7 to 16 months in STEP [pmid-40272823]; within the first year in a Czech molecular series [pmid-39773640]. Late relapses exist but are rare: two stage IA OTST patients died at 64 and 69 months [pmid-40267023]; a 40-year-old relapsed more than 5 years after a tumor first called a cystadenoma [pmid-32341591]; a Turkish patient relapsed at 48 months [pmid-36748172]. The European recommendations say relapse is usually within 2 years except in adult-type tumors [pmid-42504512].

## Where sources disagree

A five-patient series saw no link between mitotic index and relapse (three of four high-index tumors did not recur) [pmid-36748172], and one guideline review cites a threshold of more than 4 mitoses as a risk factor [pmid-39877787], against the 19-20 threshold the registries use [pmid-40267023, pmid-40272823]. Whether FOXL2 expression carries prognostic weight is contested [pmid-36869369].

## What is unknown

How to measure the mitotic index reproducibly (OTST pooled central and local counts and calls for a standard) [pmid-40267023], and whether treating low-stage, high-index tumors prevents the relapses seen in both registries [pmid-40272823].
