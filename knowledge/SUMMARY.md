## Biology

Juvenile granulosa cell tumor lacks the FOXL2 p.C134W mutation that defines adult granulosa cell tumor, and is separated from it at the genome-wide methylation level [pmid-35031544]. Reported recurrent alterations include KMT2C truncating mutations, SWI/SNF complex genes, hotspot AKT1 and DICER1 mutations, and TERT rearrangements with CLPTM1L or DROSHA in about 13 percent of tumors [pmid-35031544].
