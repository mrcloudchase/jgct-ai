## Notes for future runs

- WebFetch returns a paraphrase; use the APIs above for anything cited.
- Ledger IDs: `pmid:<n>`, `doi:<lowercase doi>`, `epmc:<SRC>:<id>`, `nct:<NCT id>`, `orpha:<code>`, `url:<short>`.
- Title-keyword priorities in `fetch.py` are crude; many priority-75 "guideline" hits are unrelated (pancreatic, testicular germ cell). Screen titles and mark `skipped` with a note.
