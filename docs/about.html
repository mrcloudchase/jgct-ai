About this knowledge base

A centralized, cited, continuously maintained account of everything known about juvenile granulosa cell tumor.

Why it exists

JGCT is rare. What is known about it is scattered across a few hundred papers, conference abstracts, registry reports, guidelines and databases, in several languages, much of it behind paywalls. A family that hears the diagnosis, or a clinician who sees one case in a career, has no single place to go. This is that place. It was started by a parent whose seven-year-old daughter is being treated for JGCT.

How it is built

  • Every source is read in full. A source enters the base only after its complete text has been read, start to finish. Nothing is taken from an abstract, a summary, a citation, or memory. Sources whose full text cannot be obtained are listed under Not yet read.
  • Numbers are copied, never computed. A source that says "4 of 31" is recorded as 4 of 31. Patient counts from overlapping cohorts are linked, not summed.
  • Disagreements are recorded, not resolved. See Where sources disagree.
  • Every claim traces to its source. Each bracketed citation opens a page showing exactly what was taken from that source and where its full text was read.
  • It is maintained by an AI agent and judged by a human. Once a month an agent reads the whole base, searches for everything new about JGCT, reads it in full, and opens a pull request. The maintainer reviews every change before it is published. The agent never rates its own work; every record is a draft until a person has checked it against its source.

What it is not

It is not medical advice, and it does not recommend treatment for anyone. It reports what published sources say. Decisions about a person's care belong with that person's care team, who can use this site to find the sources quickly.

Help it grow

The biggest gap is full text behind paywalls. Many of the field's foundational papers are on the Not yet read list. If you have library access or are an author of one of them, the repository explains how to help. Corrections to any record are welcome there too.
