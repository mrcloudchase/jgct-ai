Where sources disagree

Pairs of sources whose findings conflict. The base records both and settles nothing; each source's page shows what it reports. Read the notes on each record for the specifics.

Francis JC 2013

MicroRNAs and Recent Insights into Pediatric Ovarian Cancers.

Review of pediatric ovarian cancers with emphasis on microRNA biology; states that about 40% of ovarian tumors in the young are germ cell tumors and 25% sex cord-stromal tumors, and that 2-8% of pediatric adnexal masses are ultimately cancer.

Secondary review. FOXL2 mutation position given as 'position 412 (C to G)' in the paper (commonly written c.402C>G); recorded as written. Journal, first author and DOI are not stated in the fetched text.

Trecourt A 2023

Relevance of Molecular Pathology for the Diagnosis of Sex Cord-Stromal Tumors of the Ovary: A Narrative Review.

Narrative review (literature searched July-October 2023, PubMed Central and Google Scholar) of ovarian sex cord-stromal tumors organized by predominant cell morphology into five groups, with diagnostic algorithms; sex cord-stromal tumors are about 8% of ovarian tumors.

Secondary review; frequencies are the cited literature figures (many JGCT molecular figures derive from a small number of earlier series, so patient overlap with those series is likely). Ovarian tumors only. DOI not stated in the fetched text.

Schultz KAP 2017

DICER1-related Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor and gynandroblastoma: Clinical and genetic findings from the International Ovarian and Testicular Stromal Tumor Registry.

International Ovarian and Testicular Stromal Tumor (OTST) Registry report on the first 107 enrolled individuals (December 2011 to March 2016) with ovarian sex cord-stromal tumors: 49 SLCT, 25 JGCT, 5 gynandroblastoma, 10 adult granulosa cell tumors and others; pathology centrally reviewed in 98/107 (local and central diagnoses agreed in 80/98, 82%).

Registry enrollment may favor referral of difficult or advanced cases; for 5 JGCT no pathologic material was available and the local diagnosis was used. Stage counts in Table 4 (7+1+4+4+3+6) sum to 25; status NED 21, relapse 5 (some relapsed patients later NED). Likely overlap with other OTST Registry papers. Journal and first author not in cached text.

Yurchenko AA 2023

Early-onset gynecological tumors in DNA repair-deficient xeroderma pigmentosum group C patients: a case series.

Case series of 5 early-onset gynecological tumors in 4 patients with xeroderma pigmentosum group C (XP-C), aged 11 to 19 years: vaginal embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma in monozygotic twins (16 years), a JGCT plus a poorly differentiated Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor (SLCT) in a 19-year-old, and a stage IC1 SLCT in an 11-year-old (aged 13 at reporting).

Discrepancies inside the paper: the text states FIGO stage IC2 for the JGCT while the abstract does not name a stage for it; the abstract says 13 years for the IC1 SLCT patient whereas tumor diagnosis was at 11 (13 is her age at reporting). Table 1 lists 'progression after 4 cycles chemo' for the JGCT, but text describes paclitaxel without serious adverse events and gives no explicit response assessment. Which tumor (JGCT or SLCT) carried each DICER1 variant is shown only in a figure not readable in the text. Journal and first author not in cached text.

Chen H 2019

RUNX3 Promotes the Tumorigenic Phenotype in KGN, a Human Granulosa Cell Tumor-Derived Cell Line.

Laboratory study of RUNX proteins in GCT cell lines (KGN, labelled adult GCT; COV434, labelled juvenile GCT; immortalized granulosa SVOG) and human GCT tissue.

Mostly about KGN (adult GCT). Text uses '15 of 25' in Results and '14 of 25 (56%)' in Discussion for the prior methylation study. COV434 identity uncertainty is stated by the authors.

Karnezis AN 2021

Re-assigning the histologic identities of COV434 and TOV-112D ovarian cancer cell lines.

COV434 was originally described as a granulosa cell tumour line from a 26-year-old woman; absence of the adult-type FOXL2 mutation and young age had led others to regard it as consistent with juvenile granulosa cell tumor.

No JGCT patients; laboratory/pathology re-review. TOV-112D is irrelevant to JGCT. The JGCT-consistent inference about COV434 originates from the cited original report, not from this study's data. Journal and first author not in fetched text.

Šafanda A 2025

Complex immunohistochemical and molecular study on 5 cases of ovarian juvenile granulosa cell tumors reveals a consistent alteration in the PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling pathway.

Of 10 cases submitted as JGCT, central expert review reclassified 4 (2 Sertoli-Leydig cell tumors, 2 sex cord-stromal tumors NOS) and 1 was a duplicate (primary of a recurrent case), leaving 5 primary JGCTs and 1 recurrence; ages 6-43 years (mean 19, median 16), FIGO IC in 4 and IIIB in 1.

Only 5 cases; includes patients aged 21 and 43 years, so not purely juvenile age. The text says all recurrences occurred within the first year and that none had hormonal features, and that 'all cases with available data presented with abdominal pain' while case 4 is listed with acute abdomen; table lists case 2 clinical data as N/A. The abstract states TERT and EP400 mutated cases both recurred, but the full text assigns TERT to case 3 with recurrence while the location of the EP400 case is not given in the text (it is in Figure 1). The patient-level genotype-to-case mapping is in Fig. 1, which is not available as text.

Kanna E 2024

A Rare Case of Juvenile Granulosa Cell Tumor in a Premenarchal Female: Clinical Presentation, Diagnosis, and Management.

A 4.5-year-old girl had 2 weeks of pubic hair and breast development; estradiol was 294.70 pg/ml (reference <27), testosterone 1.330 ng/ml (<0.250), 17-OHP 5.23 ng/ml (<1.00), DHEA-S 0.467 ug/ml (normal), FSH <0.30 and LH <0.02 mIU/ml (gonadotropin-independent); AFP and beta-hCG normal.

The statement that JGCT lacks a recognized genetic driver is the authors'; no molecular testing was done. Mitotic counts given in two units in the paper. Follow-up only 1 year. The discussion's claim of 'FOXL2 mutation expression' as recurrence risk is as printed.

Zhang T 2025

Role of adjuvant chemotherapy in stage IC ovarian granulosa cell tumors: a systematic review and meta-analysis.

PROSPERO CRD42024559478; PubMed, Embase and Cochrane searched to 1 December 2024; 703 records screened, 70 full texts assessed, 12 retrospective studies (695 stage IC GCT patients) included; 255 (36.7%) received adjuvant chemotherapy and 440 (63.3%) observation; follow-up 1-396 months.

Adult and juvenile GCT are pooled; JGCT data are a subgroup (33 patients, 5 studies). Some studies did not state type (Mangili, Nasioudis: 'GCT'). JGCT subgroup data are from case series that may overlap with other records in the knowledge base (e.g. Calaminus 1997, Ndhlovu 2021, Marino 2024 series). The abstract's 'overall recurrence 18.7%' is 50/268, not out of 695. Supplementary tables not available.

Schneider DT 2026

Ovarian Sex Cord Stromal Tumors in Children and Adolescents-The European Standard Clinical Practice Recommendations.

European Standard Clinical Practice guideline from EXPeRT/ERN PaedCan for ovarian sex cord stromal tumors (OSCST) in children and adolescents, based on national and one European cohort analyses, a systematic literature review and expert consensus; evidence mostly level III (prospective cohorts) with grades A-E; no high-level (I-II) evidence exists.

Guideline text published 2026 (accepted June 2026); full text read from an institutional repository PDF of the open-access article; figure 1 flowchart text is garbled but mirrors Table 3; supplementary Tables S1-S2 (evidence grading, chemotherapy regimens) not included in the text read. Builds on EXPeRT/PARTNER 2021 recommendations and on the STEP registry analysis in pmid-40272823 (same lead author), so the evidence overlaps.

Arnaboldi SMC 2025

Management of ovarian granulosa cell tumor in childhood: a case report and recommendations for a multidisciplinary approach.

Index case: a 10-month-old girl with bilateral breast enlargement (Tanner B3PH1A1), estrogenized genitalia, clitoromegaly and macrocephaly; estradiol 195 pg/mL, testosterone 1.64 ng/mL with FSH and LH <0.3 mIU/mL; elevated inhibin B; ultrasound 48 x 38 mm and MRI 56 x 34 x 46 mm solid pelvic mass; histology jGCT, capsule intact, pT1apNx, FIGO/WHO IA; no adjuvant chemotherapy; no recurrence at 15 months; overgrowth NGS panel and PTEN MLPA negative.

The index case report has an internal laterality inconsistency: MRI described a right iliac fossa mass 'originating from the contralateral ovary' and the figure legend says right pelvic mass originating from the left adnexa, while surgery was a left salpingo-oophorectomy. n_jgct_patients is the 94 literature cases (the index case is additional). Supplementary Tables S1-S5 and Supplementary Figure S1 were not in the text; figures were not available. Review percentages use varying denominators; its cases come from case reports (publication bias). Likely overlap with other case reports in the knowledge base cited in the review.
