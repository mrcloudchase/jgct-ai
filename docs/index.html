How to read this site

  • Every claim is cited. A bracketed code such as [pmid-40272823] is a source. Click it to see what was taken from that source and where its full text was read.
  • Nothing is summarized from an abstract. A source is in this base only if its full text was read start to finish. Sources known but not yet readable are listed under Not yet read.
  • Disagreements are shown, not settled. Where sources conflict, both are presented under Where sources disagree.
  • Browse by evidence. Genes lists every reported alteration by gene. Treatments lists every regimen and outcome reported. Cohorts lists the larger series and registries.
