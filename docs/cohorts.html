Cohorts, registries, guidelines

The sources that carry the most weight: series of five or more patients, registry reports, guidelines and meta-analyses, largest first. Patient counts are as each source states them; overlapping cohorts are linked on each source's page and must not be added together.

166Grogg J 2020
Risk factors and treatment outcomes of 239 patients with testicular granulosa cell tumors: a systematic review of published case series data.
ReviewTestisPRISMA systematic review (PROSPERO CRD42018110112; searches to 5 May 2018): 88 publications, 239 patients with testicular granulosa cell tumor (tGrCT): 166 (69%) juvenile and 73 (31%) adult.
113Schneider DT 2025
Testicular and ovarian Juvenile granulosa cell tumors in children and adolescents: Analysis of 113 patients registered to the German Registry for Rare Pediatric Tumors (STEP).
Registry reportOvary and testisBetween 2001 and 2019, 119 patients with JGCT were prospectively registered in the German STEP/MAKEI registries (centers in Germany, Austria, Switzerland); after excluding those older than 18 years, 113 were analyzed: 24 testicular and 89 ovarian; reference pathology and central therapy recommendations were used.
94Arnaboldi SMC 2025
Management of ovarian granulosa cell tumor in childhood: a case report and recommendations for a multidisciplinary approach.
ReviewOvaryIndex case: a 10-month-old girl with bilateral breast enlargement (Tanner B3PH1A1), estrogenized genitalia, clitoromegaly and macrocephaly; estradiol 195 pg/mL, testosterone 1.64 ng/mL with FSH and LH <0.3 mIU/mL; elevated inhibin B; ultrasound 48 x 38 mm and MRI 56 x 34 x 46 mm solid pelvic mass; histology jGCT, capsule intact, pT1apNx, FIGO/WHO IA; no adjuvant chemotherapy; no recurrence at 15 months; overgrowth NGS panel and PTEN MLPA negative.
70Harris AK 2025
Ovarian juvenile granulosa cell tumor: A report from the International Ovarian and Testicular Stromal Tumor and International Pleuropulmonary Blastoma/DICER1 Registries.
Registry reportOvary70 ovarian juvGCT patients enrolled in the International OTST and/or IPPB/DICER1 Registries, diagnosed 2001-2024; central pathology review for 80% (56/70); median age 10 years (range 1 day-38 years), 93% (65/70) younger than 20; median follow-up 31 months (range 0-271).
52White VM 2023

Cohort studyOvarySelf-report survey (43 items; 2014-2019) of members of the closed Granulosa Cell Tumor-Survivor Sisters Facebook group: 743 analyzed respondents (691 adult-type, 52 juvenile-type); 11 surveys were completed for a person aged 16 or younger, generally by a family member.
33Zhang T 2025
Role of adjuvant chemotherapy in stage IC ovarian granulosa cell tumors: a systematic review and meta-analysis.
Meta-analysisOvaryPROSPERO CRD42024559478; PubMed, Embase and Cochrane searched to 1 December 2024; 703 records screened, 70 full texts assessed, 12 retrospective studies (695 stage IC GCT patients) included; 255 (36.7%) received adjuvant chemotherapy and 440 (63.3%) observation; follow-up 1-396 months.
26Bergamini A 2022

ReviewOvaryNarrative literature review (PubMed 1994 to July 2021, English) on oncological and reproductive outcomes after fertility-sparing surgery in sex cord stromal tumors: obstetrical outcomes in 255 patients (132 granulosa cell tumors: 98 adult, 26 juvenile, 8 unspecified) and oncological outcomes in 900 patients from 12 publications.

Registry reportOvaryInternational Ovarian and Testicular Stromal Tumor (OTST) Registry report on the first 107 enrolled individuals (December 2011 to March 2016) with ovarian sex cord-stromal tumors: 49 SLCT, 25 JGCT, 5 gynandroblastoma, 10 adult granulosa cell tumors and others; pathology centrally reviewed in 98/107 (local and central diagnoses agreed in 80/98, 82%).

Cohort studyOvaryRetrospective cohort from the MD Anderson Rare Gynecologic Malignancy Registry (2000-March 2025): 50 mixed ovarian sex cord-stromal tumors identified; 22 excluded (15 no follow-up, 7 diagnosed before 2000); 28 analyzed, 16 with documented recurrence and 12 without (recurrence rate 57%).
14Heravi-Moussavi A 2012
Recurrent somatic DICER1 mutations in nonepithelial ovarian cancers.

All 13 JGCT were ovarian.

7Kooij CD 2020

6Yaacoub S 2024
A clinicopathological study about the epidemiology of granulosa cell tumors in Lebanon.
Cohort studyOvaryRetrospective single-center (National Institute of Pathology, Beirut) study of 65 consecutive ovarian granulosa cell tumors (GCT) referred from Lebanese hospitals January 2000 to January 2020; 6 (9.2%) juvenile and 59 (90.8%) adult type. Calculated GCT incidence 16.2 per million per year (authors describe this as upper end of international range).

COG/POG staging: stage I 12, II 2, IV 1 in the whole series.

GuidelineTestisConsensus statement of 20 genitourinary pathologists (modified Delphi, three questionnaires, live vote 3/23/2024 at Johns Hopkins) with 70% agreement threshold; testicular sex cord-stromal tumors are about 5% of testicular neoplasms, about 90% are indolent and about 10% aggressive.
