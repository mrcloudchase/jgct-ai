Juvenile granulosa cell tumour in the third trimester of pregnancy

Why it matters for JGCT

JGCT detected in the third trimester of pregnancy in an adult (26 years), with marked inhibin B elevation and mildly raised AFP; illustrates diagnostic challenge and fertility-preserving surgery.

Key findings

  • 26-year-old nulliparous woman (BMI 31) in her first pregnancy; enlarged polycystic right ovary (85 cc) was seen at the 20-week scan, but she missed follow-up and re-presented at 37 weeks with a 139 x 107 x 105 mm solid mass with cystic areas, moderate Doppler flow, footling breech and a fetus at the 5th centile.
  • Non-contrast MRI showed a 13 x 7.5 cm complex right adnexal mass with hemorrhage and no restricted diffusion, read as a presumed benign process such as torsion, conflicting with the malignant-appearing ultrasound.
  • Pre-operative markers: inhibin B 3220 pg/ml (reference 0-341), AFP 44.5 kIU/L (0-5.8), LDH 307 U/L (135-214), CA-125 55 kU/L (0-35), CA 15-3 18 kU/L (normal).
  • Caesarean section with right salpingo-oophorectomy, omental and uterine serosa biopsies and washings: 790 g tumor with extensive infarction and hemorrhage; omental and ligament biopsies and washings negative; no capsular breach; viable tumor cells at a uterine serosa adhesion site.
  • Histology: sheets of round-to-oval cells with eosinophilic or clear vacuolated cytoplasm, follicle-like spaces, easily found mitoses; IHC diffuse inhibin, CD56, pancytokeratin and SMA; focal calretinin, S100 and MNF116; PAX8, WT1, GATA3, EMA and CD10 negative.
  • Difficult classification (differential pregnancy luteoma, GCT, Sertoli-Leydig tumor); expert review (McCluggage) favored juvenile granulosa cell tumor because of architecture and intermediate-size follicles; MDT diagnosis stage IA JGCT.
  • Follow-up: inhibin B normal at <9.8 pg/ml and CT without disease at first review; 3-monthly inhibin for the first year and 10-year follow-up planned.
  • Authors discuss diagnostic difficulty in pregnancy, MRI limits, that pregnancy produces disorderly cell arrangement, edema and luteinization not usually seen in non-pregnant JGCT, and note chemotherapy is not usually given in stage IA.

Presentation

  • incidental: enlarged polycystic right ovary (85 cc) on 20-week anomaly scan; no interval care until 37 weeks
  • at 37 weeks: solid right adnexal mass 139 x 107 x 105 mm with cystic areas, colour score 3
  • pregnancy 13 weeks incidental fundal fibroid 88 x 77 x 61 mm

Treatments reported

  • Elective caesarean section with right salpingo-oophorectomy, omental and uterine serosa biopsies, peritoneal washings (37 weeks of pregnancy, stage IA; n = 1)
    Outcome: live fetus delivered without complication; no adjuvant therapy; at first follow-up inhibin normal (<9.8 pg/ml) and CT without residual disease; planned 10-year follow-up

Notes

Short follow-up reported (first follow-up only). Diagnosis relied on expert opinion; luteoma considered less likely. Elevated AFP and sheets of luteinized cells reflect the pregnancy setting per authors' discussion.
