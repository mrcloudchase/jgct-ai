Why it matters for JGCT

Infant JGCT with isosexual precocious puberty; surgery alone, as the authors recommend for early stage.

Key findings

  • Poster abstract (RGCON 2016, MMIMSR Ambala, India) reporting a rare cause of isosexual precocious puberty, ovarian juvenile granulosa cell tumour, with a brief literature review.
  • A one-year-old girl presented with abdominal mass, vaginal discharge and rapid onset of pubertal development; she underwent exploratory laparotomy with tumour resection and pathology reported juvenile granulosa cell tumour of the ovary.
  • Authors state that early-stage granulosa cell tumor treated surgically has a good prognosis and adjuvant chemotherapy is not indicated in this setting.
  • The abstract gives no hormone values, stage or follow-up.

Presentation

  • abdominal mass

  • rapid onset of pubertal development (isosexual precocious puberty)

Treatments reported

  • Exploratory laparotomy with tumour resection (1-year-old with ovarian JGCT; n = 1)
    Outcome: not reported; authors state adjuvant chemotherapy is not indicated in early-stage surgically treated disease
