BMP-specific SMADs function as novel repressors of PDGFA and modulate its expression in ovarian granulosa cells and tumors.

Why it matters for JGCT

Mechanistic evidence on BMP/SMAD1/5 loss and PDGFA in a mouse model resembling JGCT and in 4 human JGCT specimens; no clinical outcomes.

Key findings

  • Laboratory study (mouse granulosa cells, Smad1/5 double conditional knockout tumors, human COV434 cell line, four archived human JGCT samples from Texas Children's Hospital tissue bank). Not a clinical study.
  • In Smad1/5 dKO mouse tumors and peritoneal metastases, qPCR showed significant upregulation of Pdgfa, Pdgfb and Vegfa, but not Vegfb, versus wild-type granulosa cells; the model develops highly vascularized, hemorrhagic, metastatic tumors resembling human JGCT.
  • Knockdown of SMAD1 and SMAD5 raised PDGFA, but not PDGFB or VEGF, in mouse granulosa cells and in COV434 (a juvenile human granulosa cell tumor-derived line; about 2-fold PDGFA increase); the effect did not depend on BMP receptor signaling (dorsomorphin plus BMP7 did not change Pdgfa).
  • ChIP showed SMAD1/5 binding to the proximal human PDGFA promoter (three-fold enrichment); reporter assays showed SMAD1/5 repress promoter activity and antagonize Sp1 by reducing Sp1 occupancy; mutating the SMAD1/5 site abolished repression. Two-hybrid assays showed no direct SMAD1/5-Sp1 interaction.
  • In four human JGCTs (T1-T4), PDGFA, PDGFRA and PDGFRB transcripts were detected in 75% and PDGFB was not expressed; most tumors expressed BMP receptors, ligands (BMP4, BMP7) and SMAD1/SMAD5 transcripts, though functional status was unknown.
  • The authors propose that PDGFA upregulation after BR-SMAD loss is an early step in JGCT development and suggest PDGF pathway expression could make JGCT or its recurrences a candidate for tyrosine kinase inhibitor treatment; this is speculation with no treatment data.

Genetic and molecular findings

  • PDGFA / PDGFRA / PDGFRB: mRNA expression (RT-PCR) (expressed in 75% (3 of 4) human JGCTs)
    PDGFB not expressed in JGCT samples
  • SMAD1/SMAD5: low expression in COV434 vs HEK293T; knockdown raises PDGFA
    mouse Smad1/5 dKO model phenocopies human JGCT

Notes

Preclinical. The four human JGCT samples (RT-PCR only) are the only patient material; the 75% figure is 3/4. Journal, first author and DOI are not stated in the fetched text.
