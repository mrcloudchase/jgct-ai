Why it matters for JGCT

Randomized comparison of TC vs BEP in ovarian sex cord-stromal tumors, predominantly adult-type granulosa cell tumor in adults; informs chemotherapy choices for advanced SCST but contains no pediatric or JGCT-specific data.

Key findings

  • GOG-0264 (GOG Foundation/NRG Oncology; PI Jubilee Brown), randomized phase II of paclitaxel/carboplatin vs BEP in newly diagnosed stage IIA-IV or recurrent chemonaive ovarian sex cord-stromal tumors; start 2010-02-08; 63 patients enrolled (31 vs 32) of 128 planned; all female, age 20 or older (mean 47.33 years, SD 13.53).
  • Cell types enrolled: granulosa cell tumor 55 (28 vs 27), Sertoli-Leydig 3, unclassified SCST 4, lipid cell 1. Measurable disease in 23 (11 vs 12), none in 40.
  • Primary outcome, median PFS (RECIST 1.1, ITT): paclitaxel/carboplatin 27.7 months (95% CI 11.2-41.0) vs BEP 19.7 months (95% CI 10.4-52.7); median follow-up 48 months.
  • Secondary: response rate in patients with a target lesion 0.43 (95% CI 0.21-0.67; n=14) vs 0.54 (95% CI 0.29-0.77; n=13); deaths 5/31 vs 8/32; serious adverse events 6/31 vs 11/32; completed protocol cycles 23/31 vs 26/32.
  • Limitations posted: study closed at the interim analysis (planned after 20 PFS events; futility threshold HR >1.10 for paclitaxel/carboplatin relative to BEP) and because of poor accrual, with 63 of 128 patients enrolled.
  • Inhibin A and B changes were a pre-specified other outcome but results are not posted. Juvenile granulosa cell tumor is not a listed condition; participants were adults.

Treatments reported

  • Paclitaxel plus carboplatin IV day 1 every 21 days x 6 courses (arm I) (newly diagnosed stage IIA-IV or recurrent chemonaive ovarian SCST; n = 31)
    Outcome: Median PFS 27.7 months (95% CI 11.2-41.0); response rate 0.43 (95% CI 0.21-0.67) in 14 with target lesions; 5 deaths; 23/31 completed 6 cycles
  • Bleomycin day 1, etoposide phosphate and cisplatin days 1-5, every 21 days x 4 courses (arm II, BEP) (same; n = 32)
    Outcome: Median PFS 19.7 months (95% CI 10.4-52.7); response rate 0.54 (95% CI 0.29-0.77) in 13 with target lesions; 8 deaths; 26/32 completed 4 cycles

Notes

Registry results record; a derived publication is cited (PMID 39265466) but was not read for this record. Overall survival is reported only as death counts. Enrollment 63 vs planned 128.
