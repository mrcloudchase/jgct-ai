Why it matters for JGCT

Short case of peripheral precocious puberty from ovarian JGCT cured by surgery.

Key findings

  • 4-year-old girl with prolonged vaginal bleeding, breast budding and abdominal enlargement; elevated estradiol and a left adnexal mass.
  • Surgical excision of the tumor led to cessation of vaginal bleeding and normalization of estradiol; pathology reported a juvenile granulosa tumor of the ovary.
  • The abstract notes that in girls with peripheral precocious puberty a common etiology is an ovarian tumor; no estradiol values, stage, or follow-up duration are given.
