Recurrent gynandroblastoma of the ovary with germline DICER1 mutation: A case report and review of the literature.

Why it matters for JGCT

Shows that an apparent JGCT in a teenager was retrospectively a gynandroblastoma with germline DICER1, supporting DICER1 testing when a JGCT has a Sertoli-Leydig component or recurs.

Key findings

  • A 24-year-old (G2P2) woman with a history of left ovarian JGCT diagnosed at 15 after laparoscopic cystectomy with intraoperative cyst rupture; she then had left salpingo-oophorectomy, partial bladder resection and excision of abdominal wall peritoneal metastasis for stage IIC JGCT, followed by 4 cycles of bleomycin, etoposide and cisplatin.
  • At 24 she presented with worsening hirsutism and irregular menses; inhibin B rose from stable values under 10 to 12 and then 89; CT showed a 5 x 5 cm septated multiloculated right adnexal mass.
  • Surgery found a ruptured cystic right ovarian mass with right common and lower para-aortic lymphadenopathy; pathology showed recurrent gynandroblastoma (intermediately differentiated Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor component plus JGCT component with variably sized cysts); inhibin and calretinin positive, reticulin lost in the JGCT-like areas; lymph nodes and omentum were free of tumor.
  • Review of the original 2012 slides showed similar findings, suggesting the first tumor was in fact a gynandroblastoma.
  • Tumor DICER1 testing showed biallelic loss-of-function and RNase IIIb missense mutations; germline testing found DICER1 c.2488G>T (p.E830*) and an NBN variant of unknown significance; both children carry the DICER1 variant.
  • From the literature review: AKT1 mutations are reported in most JGCTs and FOXL2 in adult GCT; seven gynandroblastomas with a JGCT component have been described; the OTST registry median age for DICER1 ovarian tumors is 16.9 years; authors recommend DICER1 germline testing for ovarian SLCT or gynandroblastoma, with consensus screening (pelvic ultrasound every 6-12 months from age 8-10 to 40).

Presentation

Genetic and molecular findings

  • DICER1: germline c.2488G>T p.E830* (stop codon); tumor with biallelic loss-of-function and RNase IIIb missense mutations (1/1)
    Both of the patient's children carry the germline variant
  • NBN: p.N256S variant of unknown significance (1/1)
    Germline 23-gene panel

Treatments reported

  • Left salpingo-oophorectomy, partial bladder resection, excision of abdominal wall peritoneal disease, then 4 cycles BEP (initial stage IIC JGCT at age 15; n = 1)
    Outcome: Recovered, two children; recurrence about 9 years later as gynandroblastoma
  • Total abdominal hysterectomy, right salpingo-oophorectomy, omentectomy, pelvic and para-aortic lymphadenectomy (recurrence; n = 1)
    Outcome: Nodes and omentum free of disease; no further follow-up reported

Notes

Case was originally diagnosed as pure JGCT; reclassification rests on re-review of slides. The text gives 2012 as the year of the original surgery. Journal and DOI not stated in the retrieved text.
