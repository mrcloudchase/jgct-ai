Why it matters for JGCT

Documents a diagnostic pitfall: an ovarian tumor in a 12-year-old first diagnosed as JGCT was SCCOHT with patchy inhibin staining; the case is not a JGCT, but informs JGCT differential and the value of SMARCA4/BRG1 testing.

Key findings

  • A 12-year-old girl (mild von Willebrand disease) with a ruptured 22 cm left ovarian tumor was initially diagnosed with juvenile granulosa cell tumor at an outside hospital and treated with debulking and four cycles of etoposide, ifosfamide and cisplatin.
  • A pelvic recurrence appeared 9 months later; CA-125 89 U/mL, while inhibin, AFP, hCG and calcium were normal.
  • The recurrence showed sheets and nests of mitotically active small blue cells with cysts and focal mucinous differentiation; EMA, mucicarmine, WT1, CD56 positive; inhibin and calretinin patchy; strong p53; ER equivocal; PR and Melan A negative.
  • Differential considered JGCT, mixed sex cord stromal tumor with heterologous elements, and SCCOHT; the diagnosis was revised to small cell carcinoma of the ovary, hypercalcemic type (SCCOHT) after NGS found SMARCA4 p.Arg381Ter (germline) and an in-frame TP53 deletion, with 46,XX karyotype and CN-LOH on SNP array.
  • The authors report this as the first SCCOHT with patchy inhibin positivity, a pitfall because inhibin is considered a way to separate JGCT (positive) from SCCOHT (negative); they note EMA positivity has also been described in some JGCT and normocalcemia does not exclude SCCOHT.
  • Authors recommend SMARCA4 sequencing or BRG1 IHC for high-grade small round blue cell tumors of the ovary, and germline testing when SMARCA4 mutation is found; the family declined genetic counseling follow-up.
  • Outcome: after additional chemotherapy and autologous stem cell transplant the patient died 5 months after the revised diagnosis from transplant complications.

Presentation

  • periumbilical abdominal pain
  • 22 cm left ovarian tumor with spontaneous rupture
  • normocalcemic
  • pelvic recurrence 9 months after initial diagnosis, CA-125 89 U/mL

Genetic and molecular findings

  • SMARCA4: c.1141C>T p.Arg381Ter nonsense; heterozygous germline; CN-LOH of 19p13.3-p13.2 as second hit (1/1 (VAF 88%))
    Diagnosis revised from JGCT to SCCOHT
  • TP53: c.594_611del18 p.Gly199_Glu204del in-frame; mosaic CN-LOH 17p13.3-p11.2 (1/1 (VAF 25.9%))
    strong diffuse p53 IHC

Treatments reported

  • Initial: debulking + 4 cycles etoposide, ifosfamide, cisplatin (as JGCT) (primary; n = 1)
    Outcome: pelvic recurrence at 9 months
  • Resection of recurrence, then bevacizumab, paclitaxel, carboplatin and autologous stem cell transplant (recurrence; n = 1)
    Outcome: died 5 months after SCCOHT diagnosis of transplant complications
