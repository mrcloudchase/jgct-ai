N-cadherin Expression in Testicular Germ Cell and Gonadal Stromal Tumors.

Why it matters for JGCT

Minimal: one testicular JGCT with N-cadherin IHC result; no clinical data.

Key findings

  • Immunohistochemical study of N-cadherin in 105 testicular germ cell tumors and 12 gonadal stromal tumors from the Armed Forces Institute of Pathology Testicular Tumor Registry; stromal tumors were 6 Sertoli cell, 4 Leydig cell, 1 Sertoli-Leydig and 1 juvenile granulosa cell tumor.
  • The single testicular juvenile granulosa cell tumor showed mainly weak cytoplasmic N-cadherin positivity (focal, variable intensity); the figure legend describes membranous and cytoplasmic staining. No patient age, clinical or outcome data are given.
  • Nine of the 12 gonadal stromal tumors were positive to a variable extent; Sertoli cell tumors 4/6 positive (weak to moderate membranous), Leydig cell tumors 3/4 weakly positive.
  • Main finding (germ cell tumors): N-cadherin was positive in 82/83 (98.7%) yolk sac tumors, typically strong, and authors propose it as an additional marker for yolk sac tumor, while embryonal carcinoma was largely negative.

Genetic and molecular findings

  • N-cadherin (CDH2), protein by IHC: weak, mainly cytoplasmic positivity (figure legend: membranous and cytoplasmic) (1/1 testicular JGCT)
    stained focally with varying intensity

Notes

n=1 limits interpretation. Abstract states 9 of 12 stromal tumors positive; Table 4 lists only Sertoli and Leydig tumors, with the JGCT and Sertoli-Leydig case described in text. Journal and first author not in retrieved text.
