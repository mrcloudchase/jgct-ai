Why it matters for JGCT

Public genomic database view of granulosa cell tumors: only one patient is explicitly labeled JGCT (no mutations called in the tumor); FOXL2 C134W dominates adult-type samples and the FOXL2-wild-type, AKT1-insertion GRCT in a 7-year-old is consistent with a JGCT profile but is not labeled juvenile by the database.

Key findings

  • Re-run this session of a scan of all 551 public cBioPortal studies (attributes ONCOTREE_CODE, CANCER_TYPE_DETAILED, CANCER_TYPE) found the label 'Juvenile Granulosa Cell Tumor' on exactly one sample: P-0013654-N01 in msk_ch_ped_2021 (a normal/blood sample from a clonal hematopoiesis study; patient AGE 6.611909866, female, White; CHEMO_TX, CISPLATIN_TX, PLATINUM_TX, TOPO_II_TX all Yes; no mutations listed).
  • The same patient's tumor sample P-0013654-T01-IM5 in msk_impact_50k_2026 is labeled only 'Granulosa Cell Tumor' (GRCT): AGE_AT_DX 6, primary site ovary, IMPACT410 panel, MUTATION_COUNT 0, FRACTION_GENOME_ALTERED 0.0956, OS_STATUS 0:LIVING, OS_MONTHS 50.597.
  • msk_impact_50k_2026 holds 58 GRCT samples from 56 patients (ages 1 to 77 years); FOXL2 C134W is present in 47 samples. OncoTree has no juvenile subtype code under GRCT, so juvenile and adult cases share the GRCT label.
  • Young GRCT samples in msk_impact_50k_2026 (all labeled GRCT, not juvenile): P-0039024-T01-IM6 age 7, ovary, AKT1 R76_C77insLKTERPRPNTFIIR plus TP53 X293_splice, living at 26.466 months; P-0064771-T01-IM7 age 1, ovary, no mutations called, 2.992 months; P-0061283-T01-IM7 age 16, ovary, no mutations called, 1.973 months; P-0022880-T01-IM6 age 7, primary site Skin, PTPRD T1450R, 21.567 months; all living at last follow-up. Two samples of patient P-0021806 (age 22, ovary primary, metastases) carry BMPR1A G298D (and AXIN1 R723* in one).
  • Other studies with GRCT-labeled samples: msk_ch_2023 (73 samples/patients, all blood normal samples with no mutations listed, FOXL2 not detected), msk_impact_2017 (14 samples, 13 patients; FOXL2 C134W in 12), pan_origimed_2020 (4 samples labeled 'Ovarian Granulosa Cell Tumor', ages 45 to 55, FOXL2 C134W in 3), pog570_bcgsc_2020 (2 samples, ages 52 and 55, both FOXL2 C134W, both deceased), ccle_broad_2019 (2), ccle_broad_2025 (1), ccle_genentech_2014 (1).
  • AACR GENIE cBioPortal requires login (HTTP 401 in the earlier session) and was not queried.

Genetic and molecular findings

  • AKT1: R76_C77insLKTERPRPNTFIIR (in-frame insertion) (1 GRCT sample (P-0039024-T01-IM6), AGE_AT_DX 7, with TP53 X293_splice)
    Labeled GRCT, not juvenile, in the database; FOXL2 wild-type
  • FOXL2: C134W (47/58 GRCT samples in msk_impact_50k_2026 (all ages); also 12/14 msk_impact_2017, 3/4 pan_origimed_2020, 2/2 pog570_bcgsc_2020)
    Not detected (no mutations called) in the single JGCT-labeled patient's tumor sample P-0013654-T01-IM5

Notes

Counts are tallies of API rows retrieved on 2026-10-30 at https://www.cbioportal.org/api, not numbers published by authors, and will change. n_jgct_patients=1 counts only the explicitly labeled patient. Reading unlabeled young-patient GRCT samples as JGCT is an inference. IMPORTANT TOOL BUG: tools/cbio_grct_scan.py swallows request errors silently; two runs of it this session returned only 1 and 77 hit rows because requests failed (the first run omitted msk_impact_50k_2026 entirely), whereas a scan with retries (/tmp/scripts/scan2.py, 0 failures) returned 278 rows across 8 studies matching the counts above; the tool should retry and report failures. The msk_ch_2023 study was not in the previous version of this record. Panel sequencing (MSK-IMPACT) does not detect all alterations; 'no mutations called' does not exclude other events.
