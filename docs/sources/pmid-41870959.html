Why it matters for JGCT

Minor: negative FRalpha immunohistochemistry in 4 JGCTs within a large series that is otherwise about epithelial tumors; no clinical data for the JGCT cases.

Key findings

  • Tissue-microarray immunohistochemistry study of 923 tubo-ovarian and endometrial tumors (796 tubo-ovarian, 127 endometrial) with the VENTANA FOLR1 CDx assay; sex cord-stromal tumors numbered 338: 249 adult GCT, 4 JGCT, 35 Sertoli-Leydig tumors and 50 fibromas/thecomas.
  • All 4 JGCTs were FRalpha negative (0/4; range and H-score 0). All other sex cord-stromal tumors were also negative (AGCT 0/249, SLT 0/35, F/T 0/50).
  • FRalpha positivity was highest in serous tumors: HGSC 47/104 (45%), LGSC 22/87 (25%), endometrial serous carcinoma 3/27 (11%), serous borderline tumor 3/30 (10%); endometrioid 1/47 and clear cell 1/86 ovarian carcinomas positive; mucinous tumors negative.
  • The authors state that sex cord-stromal tumors were analyzed for the first time in this series and that FRalpha-targeted therapy (mirvetuximab soravtansine, approved for platinum-resistant HGSC) has only been studied in HGSC; no role for it in JGCT is shown.

Genetic and molecular findings

  • FOLR1 (FRalpha protein): immunohistochemistry, VENTANA FOLR1 CDx (positive = 2+/3+ in >=75% tumor cells) (JGCT 0/4 positive; adult GCT 0/249; Sertoli-Leydig 0/35; fibroma/thecoma 0/50)
    H-score 0 in all four JGCTs
