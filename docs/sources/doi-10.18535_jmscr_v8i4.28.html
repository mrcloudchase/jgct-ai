Why it matters for JGCT

Case of a 19-year-old with an androgenic presentation and large JGCT with focal Sertoli-Leydig-like areas; relevant to differential diagnosis and the use of reticulin stain.

Key findings

  • 19-year-old woman with hirsutism, clitoromegaly and oligomenorrhea (cycles every 45-60 days) and a palpable pelvic-origin abdominal mass.
  • CT and MRI: cystic left ovarian mass, thick-walled and multiloculated, with minimal ascites. Estradiol 400 pg/mL (reference 63.9-356.7), FSH 0.40 and LH 2.00 mIU/mL (below reference ranges); AFP, hCG, CEA, CA 19-9 and inhibin B within normal limits.
  • Unilateral salpingo-oophorectomy specimen: 22 x 15 x 6 cm ovarian mass (tube 8.5 cm), solid grey-white cut surface with small mucinous cysts.
  • Histology: follicles with eosinophilic material between sheets of round to polyhedral cells with hyperchromatic nuclei and occasional grooves, luteinized cells, thecomatous/fibromatous stroma, mitotic index 3 per HPF, marked necrosis and hemorrhage; focal areas resembling poorly differentiated Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor.
  • Reticulin stain showed a paucity of reticulin in thecomatous foci (granulosa nature) with reticulin surrounding tumor cell nests; IHC positive for vimentin, CD99, CD56 and cytokeratin AE1/AE3 (inhibin result not stated). Diagnosis: JGCT; omentum free of tumor.
  • Authors' conclusions: JGCT is uncommon, accurate histopathologic diagnosis is key, and fertility-sparing surgery is appropriate; their discussion notes this patient's age and androgenic presentation differ from the typical prepubertal estrogenic presentation.

Presentation

  • excessive body hair (hirsutism)
  • clitoromegaly
  • irregular menstruation (3-4 days every 45-60 days)
  • non-tender pelvic/abdominal mass

Treatments reported

  • Unilateral salpingo-oophorectomy (19-year-old, 22 x 15 x 6 cm left ovarian mass; n = 1)
    Outcome: omentum free of tumor; no follow-up reported

Notes

Inhibin B serum level was normal, and tumor inhibin IHC was not reported. Focal poorly differentiated Sertoli-Leydig-like areas raise a possible mixed sex cord-stromal tumor; the authors diagnosed JGCT. No follow-up reported. Mitotic index 3 per HPF as written.
