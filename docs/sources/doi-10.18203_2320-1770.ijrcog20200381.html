Gynandroblastoma: a rare case report of ovarian sex cord tumour

Why it matters for JGCT

Case of ovarian gynandroblastoma with a juvenile granulosa cell component in a postmenopausal woman; limited JGCT-specific information (histology, inhibin positivity, no recurrence at 2 years).

Key findings

  • 61-year-old postmenopausal woman with an abdominopelvic mass and no virilization; ultrasound showed a latero-uterine mass of 27.8 cm, predominantly cystic with a partial solid component; CT showed a left adnexal heterogeneous cystic mass with a peripheral solid component.
  • AFP 2.53 ng/mL, CEA 2.43 ng/mL, CA19-9 3.19 U/mL and CA125 30 U/mL were within normal range (hCG not measured); hormonal work-up (estrogen, testosterone) was not done.
  • Laparotomy showed no peritoneal implants and negative peritoneal washing; left adnexectomy was done with biopsies; specimen weighed 4500 g with two cystic masses (25 x 20 x 15 cm and 20 x 13 x 6 cm) joined by a firm pale-yellow nodular area.
  • Histology: mixed tumor with Sertoli component (tubules lined by 2-3 cell layers), Leydig cell nests and juvenile granulosa cells; juvenile granulosa cells and Sertoli-Leydig cells were alpha-inhibin positive; diagnosis gynandroblastoma. Completion total hysterectomy, right adnexectomy and omentectomy were benign.
  • Close observation without adjuvant chemotherapy (clinical review every 3 months for 2 years, markers every 6 months, yearly imaging years 2-5); at 2 years inhibin A, inhibin B and AFP negative with no suspicious imaging.
  • Literature cited: a meta-analysis of 22 gynandroblastoma cases reported no recurrence over 6 months to 13 years, with unilateral adnexectomy in 17/22; tumors mostly stage I; the authors call for a registry to define management.

Presentation

  • abdominopelvic mass, 27.8 cm, predominantly cystic with partial solid component
  • no virilization or hirsutism

Treatments reported

  • Left adnexectomy with biopsies, then total hysterectomy, right adnexectomy and omentectomy; no adjuvant chemotherapy (gynandroblastoma with juvenile granulosa component; n = 1)
    Outcome: no recurrence at 2 years; AFP, inhibin A and inhibin B negative; imaging unremarkable

Notes

Tumor is a mixed gynandroblastoma, not pure JGCT; patient aged 61, unusual for JGCT. Pathology details are limited (no further IHC beyond inhibin). The paper's abstract says initial unilateral adnexectomy then completion hysterectomy. Follow-up only 2 years.
