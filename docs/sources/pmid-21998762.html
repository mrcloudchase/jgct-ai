Clinicopathologic characteristics of granulosa cell tumors of the ovary: a multicenter retrospective study.

Why it matters for JGCT

Multicenter GCT series with a clearly reported 11-patient JGCT subgroup (presentation, stage, surgery, chemotherapy, outcome, pregnancies); the adult-type findings are context only.

Key findings

  • Five-hospital Korean retrospective study (1995-2007) of 113 granulosa cell tumors: 102 adult type and 11 juvenile type (9.7%).
  • JGCT patients: mean age 20.0 years (range 8-45), more than half under 20, 5/11 (45.5%) premenarchal; 10 of 11 symptomatic; most common symptom abdominal mass (45.5%), vaginal bleeding in 3 (27.3%).
  • JGCT tumors: mean diameter 12.4 cm (range 5-26), all unilateral (right 7, left 4); preoperative CA-125 was elevated (>35 IU/mL) in 5 of the 10 measured.
  • JGCT surgery: unilateral salpingo-oophorectomy with staging 6, unilateral salpingo-oophorectomy 3, hysterectomy with bilateral salpingo-oophorectomy 1, cystectomy 1; intraoperative rupture in 3 (27.3%); no capsule invasion; no residual disease; no positive ascites or washings.
  • JGCT stage: stage I in 9 (81.8%) and stage III in 2 (18.2%); 3 patients received adjuvant BEP.
  • JGCT outcome: no recurrences or deaths at mean follow-up 69.8 months (range 20-156); four patients had seven pregnancies, six normal term deliveries and one cornual pregnancy.
  • Adult-type comparison (102 pts): stage I 86, II 11, III 5; recurrence 10 (9.8%); 3 deaths; stage the only factor independently associated with recurrence; 8 of 36 fertility-sparing patients had 9 pregnancies.
  • Authors conclude JGCT prognosis is good in their small group and fertility-sparing surgery is an option for early-stage disease.

Presentation

  • JGCT: abdominal mass (45.5%)
  • vaginal bleeding (3 patients, 27.3%)
  • abdominal pain (2 patients)
  • 5/11 (45.5%) premenarchal
  • ascites in 3/11
  • only one JGCT patient asymptomatic

Treatments reported

  • Surgery: unilateral SO with staging 6/11, unilateral SO 3/11, hysterectomy + BSO 1/11, cystectomy 1/11 (primary; n = 11)
    Outcome: No recurrences or deaths; mean follow-up 69.8 months (range 20-156)
  • Adjuvant BEP (JGCT) (adjuvant; n = 3)
    Outcome: No recurrence; discussion says given to the two stage III patients (one stage IC patient also treated, one not)

Notes

Journal and first author were not present in the fetched text. Internal errors in the JGCT paragraph: percentages for pain (2 patients labelled 28.3%), left-sided 4 labelled 50.0%, '3 patients (32.3%)' for chemotherapy (3/11 is 27.3%). Adult-type text has also '843.3%' typo and a mean recurrence interval of 48 months with range 4-92 vs 4-109 in the abstract. Authors state they treated stage III JGCT with adjuvant chemotherapy; stage IC: 1 treated, 1 not (this implies 2 IC among the 9 stage I).
