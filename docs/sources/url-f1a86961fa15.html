Precocious puberty and ovarian tumors - 2 case reports

Why it matters for JGCT

Peripheral precocious puberty from JGCT with estradiol above 1000 pg/ml and normalisation after oophorectomy; contrasted with a mixed sex cord-stromal tumor.

Key findings

  • Two-case abstract of peripheral precocious puberty from ovarian tumors; only case 1 was a juvenile granulosa cell tumor (JGCT).
  • Case 1: 3-year-old girl, breast enlargement over 1 year, irregular vaginal bleeding for 10 months, pubic hair for 2 months, Tanner B3P3, firm mobile pelvic mass in right lower abdomen; bone age 8 years 10 months vs chronological age 3 years.
  • Case 1 hormones: LH <0.1 and FSH <0.1 mIU/ml, testosterone 0.334 ng/ml, DHEAS 17.97 ug/dl, estradiol >1000 pg/ml; CT showed a heterogeneously enhancing abdomino-pelvic mass without separately visualised ovaries; resected right tubo-ovarian mass was juvenile granulosa cell tumor; estradiol fell to 17 pg/ml; no vaginal bleeding at 1 year.
  • Case 2: 6-year-old girl, breast enlargement over 7 months, cyclical vaginal bleeding for 4 months, Tanner B3P3, bone age 7.8 years, estradiol 162.8 pg/ml (text also gives 162.9), CT showed a bulky 5.4 cm left ovary; laparoscopic resection of the 'right' tubo-ovarian mass showed a mixed germ cell sex cord stromal tumor; estradiol 14.37 pg/ml afterwards; no bleeding at 6 months.
  • Authors conclude the cases illustrate heterogeneity in presentation, biochemistry and histopathology; both had successful outcomes without recurrence (12 and 6 months follow-up as stated).

Presentation

  • progressive bilateral breast enlargement over 1 year
  • irregular vaginal bleeding for 10 months
  • pubic hair for 2 months (Tanner B3P3)
  • palpable right lower abdominal pelvic mass

Treatments reported

  • Resection of right tubo-ovarian mass (3-year-old with JGCT; n = 1)
    Outcome: estradiol fell from >1000 to 17 pg/ml; no vaginal bleeding at 1 year follow-up

Notes

Whole abstract read (PMC3850124). Internal inconsistencies: case 2 CT shows a left ovary but resection is described as right tubo-ovarian; the abstract text gives follow-up as 'one year' for case 1 in the case text but '12 months' in the conclusion. n_jgct_patients counts case 1 only.
