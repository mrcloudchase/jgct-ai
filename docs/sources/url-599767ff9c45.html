Why it matters for JGCT

The registry that underlies several JGCT-relevant reports (including the OTST conference abstracts); it explicitly lists juvenile granulosa cell tumor among its tumor types and enrolls patients, families and biospecimens.

Key findings

  • International OTST Registry (Children's Hospitals and Clinics of Minnesota; PI Kris Ann Schultz) collects clinical and biologic data on ovarian and testicular stromal tumors; status RECRUITING (verified 2026-10); start 2011-12-08; estimated enrollment 500; primary completion estimated 2030-12-31.
  • Arms: ovarian stromal tumors, testicular stromal tumors, ovarian small cell carcinoma; keywords include juvenile granulosa cell tumor, Sertoli, Leydig, gynandroblastoma. Eligibility: all patients with a suspected gonadal stromal tumor or similar tumor, any age, most expected under 20.
  • Rationale stated: ovarian sex cord-stromal tumors have been associated with pleuropulmonary blastoma/DICER1; DICER1 mutations are seen in some patients with ovarian tumors; ovarian stromal tumors are highly curable when found at early stage but later-stage forms are aggressive and often fatal.
  • Six registry goals: understand risk factors (age, subtype, histology, invasiveness, differentiation, metastasis); collect personal and family history incl. germline DICER1 status; relate gene expression/DNA alterations to predisposition, behavior and outcome; determine optimal screening; refine treatment algorithms; bank annotated tumor and germline specimens.
  • Enrollment is by local diagnosis with central pathology review offered; requested materials are blood for DNA and lymphoblastoid cell lines, slides/blocks/snap-frozen/fresh tumor; cases come from clinician or pathologist referral, family self-referral, and requests to authors of published cases.
  • Primary outcome: DICER1 mutations in ovarian and testicular stromal tumors (germline and tumor sequencing, mRNA/miRNA expression correlated with outcome), 3 years; secondary: clinical factors associated with outcome (10 years) and pathologic/genetic correlations (5 years). No results posted.

Registry listing only; no results posted. Reference list in the record includes OTST registry publications (e.g. PMIDs 26289771, 29037807, 38657450).
