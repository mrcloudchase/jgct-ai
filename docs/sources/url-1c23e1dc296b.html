Atypical presentations of dicer1 syndrome in adults with juvenile granulosa cell tumor

Why it matters for JGCT

Two adult JGCT cases with germline DICER1 variants, supporting testing for DICER1 in JGCT.

Key findings

  • Two women (20 and 25 years old) had complex abdominal masses on imaging; final pathology after exploratory laparotomy with mass removal and staging was juvenile granulosa cell tumor (JGCT) in both.
  • Both then had genetic testing showing heterozygous germline DICER1 pathogenic variants: patient 1 DICER1 c.556delT (all three of her male children were also found to carry it); patient 2 DICER1 c.2988-20_2988del, with malignant follicular thyroid carcinoma diagnosed at age 11.
  • Family histories of both suggested undiagnosed DICER1 syndrome in multiple relatives.
  • Authors conclude the association of JGCTs with DICER1 syndrome may be underrecognized and underreported; they describe JGCT as rarely seen with this syndrome.

Presentation

  • complex abdominal masses on imaging after clinical evaluation of symptoms

Genetic and molecular findings

  • DICER1: c.556delT (germline, patient 1) (1/2 patients)
    all three male children of patient 1 carry the same variant
  • DICER1: c.2988-20_2988del (germline, patient 2) (1/2 patients)
    patient 2 had malignant follicular thyroid carcinoma at age 11

Treatments reported

  • Exploratory laparotomy with mass removal and staging (adult JGCT with germline DICER1; n = 2)
    Outcome: not reported
