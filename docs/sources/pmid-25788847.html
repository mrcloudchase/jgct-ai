Why it matters for JGCT

Small adult-and-adolescent series showing excellent outcomes and preserved fertility after fertility-sparing surgery for stage I JGCT.

Key findings

  • Retrospective single-centre (Etlik Zubeyde Hanim hospital, Ankara) series: 10 JGCT among 143 granulosa cell tumors (7%) diagnosed April 1995 to January 2014; median age 21.5 years (range 13-36).
  • Presenting symptoms: bloating in 5, abdominal pain in 4, menometrorrhagia in 1; CA-125 measured in 5, normal in 4, 74 U/mL in the patient with a ruptured cyst; ascites in 4; median tumor diameter 140 mm (60-400); right side 5, left side 5.
  • FIGO 1988 stage IA in 9 and stage IC in 1 (preoperative rupture, negative cytology); all tumors completely resected; 8 had unilateral salpingo-oophorectomy, 1 cystectomy, 1 TAH+BSO elsewhere (re-staged).
  • Five patients had pelvic and para-aortic lymphadenectomy (median 54 nodes, range 7-94), none positive; omentectomy in 7 (6 infracolic, 1 partial), appendectomy in 2, no omental tumor.
  • Three patients had adjuvant therapy: BEP x4 (stage IC), VAC x6 (stage IA) and radiotherapy (stage IA); frozen section was diagnostic for JGCT in 2 of 5, sex cord-stromal tumor NOS in 2, lymphoma suspected in 1.
  • No recurrence in median follow-up 58 months (range 3-113). Of 5 patients desiring pregnancy, 4 had 5 term pregnancies after fertility-sparing surgery, including the stage IC patient treated with BEP.
  • Authors conclude surgery is primary management, adjuvant therapy benefit is unclear, and fertility-sparing surgery can be safely suggested in early stage; they suggest lymphadenectomy may be unnecessary in sex cord-stromal tumors and recommend long follow-up with inhibin B.

Treatments reported

  • Unilateral salpingo-oophorectomy (8), cystectomy (1), TAH+BSO (1) (surgery, all completely resected; n = 10)
    Outcome: no recurrence; median follow-up 58 months (3-113)
  • Adjuvant bleomycin-etoposide-cisplatin x4 cycles (stage IC; n = 1)
    Outcome: no recurrence; later live birth
  • Adjuvant vincristine-doxorubicin-cyclophosphamide x6 cycles (stage IA; n = 1)
    Outcome: no recurrence (follow-up 3 months)
  • Adjuvant radiotherapy (stage IA; n = 1)
    Outcome: no recurrence (follow-up 53 months)
  • Pelvic and para-aortic lymphadenectomy (staging; n = 5)
    Outcome: no lymph node involvement; median 54 nodes removed (7-94)

Notes

Full text is a 3-page PDF (abstract appears mid-text). Mostly young adults (mean ~22 years) treated in a gynecologic oncology clinic.
