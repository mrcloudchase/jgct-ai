Clinical utility of SMARCA4 testing by immunohistochemistry in rare ovarian tumours.

Why it matters for JGCT

Important for differential diagnosis: JGCT is the most frequent misdiagnosis of SCCOHT in this series (6/16), and SMARCA4 IHC (retained in JGCT) helps separate them. JGCT numbers (n=10) are the comparator group.

Key findings

  • French multicenter (Gustave Roussy and Hospices Civils de Lyon) study of SMARCA4 immunohistochemistry in 44 centrally confirmed SCCOHT and 94 rare ovarian tumors that mimic SCCOHT, including 10 juvenile granulosa cell tumors (Methods: 34 adult GCT; Table 1: 37 adult GCT).
  • SMARCA4 loss in SCCOHT 39/44 (88%) vs 6/94 (6%) mimickers; sensitivity 88.6% (abstract 88%; text 89%), specificity 93.6%, PPV 86.7%, NPV 94.6%, accuracy 92.0%; projected community-setting PPV 40% and NPV 99% at 5% SCCOHT prevalence.
  • The six SMARCA4-negative non-SCCOHT tumors were 3 adult granulosa cell tumors (two FOXL2-mutation confirmed), 1 immature teratoma, 1 desmoplastic round cell tumor and 1 PNET; no juvenile GCT is listed among them, so SMARCA4 IHC separated the 10 JGCT from SCCOHT (specificity in sex cord tumors 94%, 49/52 positive).
  • Diagnostic pitfall: of 36 SCCOHT reviewed as second opinion, 16 (44%; abstract says 43%) had a different initial local diagnosis; the main misdiagnosis was juvenile granulosa cell tumor (6/16, 38%).
  • SMARCA4 IHC would have corrected 14/16 (88%) of the misdiagnosed SCCOHT; two with retained SMARCA4 would not have been helped.
  • Authors recommend SMARCA4 IHC for any non-epithelial ovarian tumor in a young woman where doubt exists, while central expert review remains required.

Genetic and molecular findings

  • SMARCA4 (BRG1) protein by IHC: retained in the 10 JGCT (loss in 6 of 94 non-SCCOHT tumors: 3 adult GCT, 1 immature teratoma, 1 DSRCT, 1 PNET) (JGCT 10/10 retained (inferred from the 6 listed SMARCA4-loss non-SCCOHT tumors); SCCOHT 39/44 lost)
    inference: no JGCT is listed among the 6 non-SCCOHT tumors with loss

Notes

Internal inconsistencies: adult GCT 34 (Methods) vs 37 (Table 1), DSRCT 1 vs 2, PNET 12 vs 13; sensitivity 88%/89%; misclassification 43% vs 44%. The claim that all 10 JGCT retained SMARCA4 is inferred from the listed SMARCA4-negative mimickers, not stated outright.
