Why it matters for JGCT

Describes the adult-type malignant ovarian granulosa cell tumor (peri/post-menopausal women); it is not a JGCT definition and serves only as a contrast to JGCT.

Key findings

  • Orphanet API (definition endpoint, entry dated 2026-07-02) defines ORPHA:99915 as a rare malignant sex cord stromal tumor of the ovary arising from granulosa cells, occurring in peri- and post-menopausal women.
  • Stated presentation: abnormal vaginal bleeding, abdominal pain and distension; the tumor is frequently unilateral and estrogen secreting.
  • Stated natural history: slow, with a tendency to relapse long after the initial diagnosis.
  • The definition describes the adult-type granulosa cell tumor; juvenile granulosa cell tumor is not mentioned in this entry.

Presentation

  • abnormal vaginal bleeding

Notes
