Adult granulosa cell tumours of the testis analogous to ovarian counterparts are exceptionally rare: analysis of a multicentric series and review of the literature.

Why it matters for JGCT

Adult testicular granulosa-like tumors; included for the single tumor with JGCT-like morphology and the DICER1/CTNNB1 results. No JGCT patients (n_jgct_patients 0) and no pediatric data.

Key findings

  • Twenty testicular adult granulosa cell tumors (AGCT) from nine institutions (22-71 years, median 43.5) were sequenced; 12/20 yielded results, none with FOXL2 p.Cys134Trp.
  • Three of 12 had pathogenic or likely pathogenic CTNNB1 alterations; 8/12 were wild-type for all panel genes (panel included DICER1).
  • One CTNNB1 wild-type tumor showed areas of AGCT and foci with juvenile granulosa cell tumor-like morphology (follicles/cysts lined by small ovoid cells) with a cystic component; this is the only juvenile-like finding in the series.
  • In the literature, only 2/29 bona fide testicular AGCTs carried FOXL2 p.Cys134Trp; the authors conclude most testicular tumors classified as AGCT are not analogous to ovarian AGCT and AGCT is a diagnosis of exclusion in the testis.
  • Follow-up: one patient developed metastases, four others with follow-up had no recurrence (48 and 54 months for two).

Presentation

  • Testicular mass, hydrocele or gynecomastia in adults

Genetic and molecular findings

  • FOXL2: p.Cys134Trp (0/12 sequenced testicular AGCTs; 2/29 bona fide testicular AGCTs including the literature)
    Hallmark of ovarian adult GCT
  • CTNNB1: p.Ser37Phe, p.Gly34Arg, p.Gln322_Trp338delinsArg (3/12 sequenced testicular AGCTs)
    Panel also covered DICER1, FH, KIT and MED12; no DICER1 variants reported
  • KIT / MED12: KIT p.Gly565Val and MED12 p.Asp1118Asn (variants of uncertain significance) (1/12)
    Judged unlikely relevant

Notes

Series is of adult-type tumors; the JGCT-like foci are a morphologic observation in one case with no molecular alteration. 8/20 tumors failed sequencing from poor DNA quality.
