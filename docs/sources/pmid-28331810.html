Why it matters for JGCT

Highlights a diagnostic pitfall (AFP positivity mimicking yolk sac tumor) in neonatal testicular JGCT.

Key findings

  • Term male neonate (39 weeks, birth weight 3550 g) with an enlarged smooth firm translucent left testis; ultrasound showed a 2.7 x 1.7 x 2.7 cm multicystic mass with internal flow replacing the testis; no genital ambiguity, anatomic abnormalities or sex chromosome aneuploidy.
  • Serum LDH 885 IU/L (normal 160-450), beta-hCG 48.58 IU/L (normal <=50), AFP 113,200 ng/ml (no reference range given).
  • Left radical inguinal orchiectomy on day 2 of life; 2.5 x 2.0 x 1.0 cm gray-white multicystic mass with basophilic/mucicarminophilic fluid; tumor limited to testis/epididymis without spermatic cord extension.
  • IHC positive for inhibin, calretinin, CD99, SF-4 (as written), FOXL2 and AFP; negative for CD30, CD117, D2-40, PanCK, PLAP, glypican-3, OCT3/4. AFP tumor positivity was unexplained, though clinical, gross and other IHC features fit JGCT.
  • No recurrence; at 7 months inhibin A 1 pg/ml, beta-hCG <1, AFP 41.9 ng/ml; markers followed every 3 months. Authors suggest electron microscopy and GATA6/CSF1R staining may help exclude yolk sac tumor and recommend adding them routinely.
  • From literature cited: Prepubertal Testis Tumor Registry had 395 tumors (244 yolk sac, 43 stromal, 11 JGCT); a 70-case study (Kao) reported AFP uniformly negative apart from one focal case, making this the second AFP-positive JGCT report per the authors.

Presentation

  • enlarged firm descended left testis at birth
  • 2.7 x 1.7 x 2.7 cm multicystic mass replacing the testis on ultrasound
  • no genital ambiguity

Treatments reported

  • Left radical inguinal orchiectomy on day 2 of life (neonatal testicular JGCT; n = 1)
    Outcome: no recurrence; tumor markers followed every 3 months; at 7 months inhibin A 1 pg/ml, beta-hCG <1 mIU/ml, AFP 41.9 ng/ml

Notes

Short follow-up (7 months). Serum AFP in neonates is physiologically high, no reference given in paper. 'SF-4' in the paper likely refers to SF-1 (unverified); recorded as written.
