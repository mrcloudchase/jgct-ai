Ovarian cell tumor in a child with neurofibromatosis type 1.

Why it matters for JGCT

Case of prepubertal JGCT causing pseudo-precocious puberty in a child with NF1; high inhibin B; authors report no earlier JGCT-NF1 case. Association is a single observation.

Key findings

  • 6-year-old girl with clinically diagnosed neurofibromatosis type 1 (more than six cafe-au-lait macules, axillary freckling, affected relatives) presented with 3 weeks of bilateral breast enlargement followed by vaginal bleeding, abdominal pain and distention.
  • Examination: Tanner breast stage 2, no pubic or axillary hair, hard nontender 7 x 7 cm right lower quadrant mass; bone age matched chronological age.
  • CT: well-defined heterogeneous solid right ovarian mass 7.8 x 7.4 x 6.2 cm with uterus stretched superiorly and thickened endometrium.
  • Hormones consistent with pseudo-precocious puberty: LH 0.04 mIU/L, FSH 2.17 IU/L, estradiol 152.72 pmol/L (ref 26-125), inhibin B >1300 pg/mL (ref 0-18); beta-hCG, AFP and CEA normal.
  • Right ovary removed (150 g, 9 x 6.5 x 5 cm, solid multicystic); histology predominantly JGCT type with variable follicles filled with eosinophilic fluid; cytology without malignant cells. No immunohistochemistry reported, no stage or follow-up given.
  • Authors state they found no prior reported case of JGCT associated with NF1 and that isosexual pseudo-precocious puberty from JGCT is not the usual presentation in NF1 (where central precocious puberty is common).

Presentation

  • 3 weeks of bilateral breast enlargement (Tanner 2)
  • vaginal bleeding 2 days before presentation
  • abdominal pain and distention
  • 7 x 7 cm right lower quadrant mass
  • no pubic or axillary hair; bone age equal to chronological age

Genetic and molecular findings

  • NF1: clinical diagnosis by NIH criteria (>6 cafe-au-lait macules, axillary freckling, family history); no molecular testing described (1 patient)
    family pedigree autosomal dominant

Treatments reported

  • Right oophorectomy (6-year-old with NF1, 9 x 6.5 x 5 cm, 150 g multicystic mass; n = 1)
    Outcome: cytology showed no malignant cells; no follow-up reported

Notes

Title says 'Ovarian Cell Tumor'; the text diagnoses JGCT. Histology is described as 'predominant features of JGCT type'. No IHC, staging or follow-up; NF1 not confirmed molecularly. Journal and first author not in retrieved text.
