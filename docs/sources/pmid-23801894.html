Why it matters for JGCT

Small pediatric ovarian-mass series providing two individually described JGCT cases (stage Ia, both disease-free).

Key findings

  • Retrospective review of 65 premenarchal girls operated on for ovarian masses at Seoul St. Mary's Hospital (1990-2012); 14 (21.5%) were malignant; juvenile granulosa cell tumor accounted for 2 (3.1%) of all masses and 14.3% of malignant tumors.
  • JGCT patient 1: age 9, vaginal bleeding, left ovarian cystectomy, FIGO stage Ia, no adjuvant treatment, no evidence of disease; beta-hCG under 2.
  • JGCT patient 2: age 14, abdominal distension, right adnexectomy with pelvic lymph node dissection and partial omentectomy, stage Ia, carboplatin-VP16 adjuvant, no evidence of disease; CA125 258, AFP 21, beta-hCG under 2.
  • The authors report one of the two granulosa cell tumors presented with pseudoprecocity (vaginal bleeding) and the other did not; CA125 was nonspecifically raised in the patient with a granulosa cell tumor.
  • Among all malignant tumors, 3 patients died (2 yolk sac tumors, 1 mixed germ cell tumor); both JGCT patients were disease-free at last follow-up (follow-up length not stated).
  • Authors conclude epithelial and sex cord-stromal tumors are relatively uncommon in children and their clinical behavior is unpredictable.

Presentation

  • Age 9: vaginal bleeding
  • Age 14: abdominal distension

Treatments reported

  • Left ovarian cystectomy, no adjuvant therapy (JGCT stage Ia, age 9; n = 1)
    Outcome: No evidence of disease
  • Right adnexectomy, pelvic lymph node dissection, partial omentectomy, carboplatin-VP16 (JGCT stage Ia, age 14; n = 1)
    Outcome: No evidence of disease

Notes

Table 6 gives 'Juvenile granulosa cell tumor' for cases 1 and 3, while the Discussion calls them granulosa cell tumors; the text says CA125 was raised in one granulosa cell tumor patient (case 3). Follow-up duration is not reported. The text states 'one patient had precocious puberty/pseudoprecocity' for 50% of GCT whereas Table 6 lists vaginal bleeding for case 1.
