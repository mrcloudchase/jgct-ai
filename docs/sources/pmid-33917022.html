Why it matters for JGCT

Low-yield, indirect: a review of rare gynecological cancers in which JGCT appears only within the granulosa cell tumor section, with one cited miRNA/FOXL2 cell-line comparison of juvenile versus adult types. Useful only as a pointer to preclinical miRNA work.

Key findings

  • Narrative review of microRNA (miRNA) data in rare gynecological cancers (annual incidence <6 per 100,000 women); the review reports no new data.
  • Granulosa cell tumors are described as about 5% of malignant ovarian tumors, with two distinct types, adult-type and juvenile granulosa cell tumor, but the authors state that most data refer to adult-type tumors; the review gives no JGCT-specific patient numbers or outcomes.
  • Testing for the C134W FOXL2 mutation is described as helpful for diagnosing adult-type tumors; it is not presented as a JGCT marker.
  • A comparative study cited by the review of miRNA regulation of FOXL2 in adult- versus juvenile-type granulosa cell tumors showed that reduction of the miR-17 family indirectly increased FOXL2 mRNA expression; through miRNA profiling, juvenile- and adult-derived cell lines were biologically distinct, which the authors say still needs to be addressed in vivo.
  • Cited work by Tu et al. reported that miR-10a promotes granulosa cell tumor development via the PTEN-AKT/Wnt axis and that miR-126 is a tumor suppressor in granulosa cell tumor via EGFL7; granulosa cell tumor type (adult or juvenile) is not specified in the review.
  • Cheng et al. (cited) validated six miRNAs (miR-29c-3p, miR-138-5p, miR-184, miR-204-5p, miR-328-3p, miR-501-3p) as markers for subtype classification in ovarian granulosa cell tumors; low miR-138-5p correlated with early stage and low miR-184 with recurrence in early-stage adult-type tumors.
  • DICER1 RNase IIIb domain mutations are described as common in non-epithelial ovarian tumors, with Sertoli-Leydig tumors highly characteristic of DICER1 syndrome; no JGCT DICER1 data are given.
  • For advanced or recurrent granulosa cell tumor the review states traditional chemotherapy has limited effectiveness and mentions the GOG264 trial (NCT01042522) of carboplatin/paclitaxel versus BEP in sex cord-stromal tumors.

Genetic and molecular findings

  • miR-17 family / FOXL2: reduced miR-17 family expression indirectly increased FOXL2 mRNA in a comparison of adult- and juvenile-type granulosa cell tumor
    cited study [71], cell-line based
  • miR-10a: upregulated in granulosa cell tumor; promotes tumor development via PTEN-AKT/Wnt
    granulosa cell tumor, type not specified
  • miR-126: downregulated; tumor suppressor via EGFL7/PI3K-AKT
    granulosa cell tumor, type not specified

Notes

Secondary review; JGCT-specific content is limited to a few sentences relying on cited primary studies not read in this run. Granulosa cell tumor miRNA data (miR-10a, miR-126) do not state adult or juvenile type.
