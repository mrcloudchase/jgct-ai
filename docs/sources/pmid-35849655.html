Use of staging for sex cord stromal tumours.

Why it matters for JGCT

Review of staging in ovarian SCST with several JGCT-specific statements (nodal metastasis, cystectomy recurrence, laparoscopy, staging). Only secondary data; no original patients.

Key findings

  • Narrative review of surgical staging of ovarian sex cord-stromal tumors (SCST), mainly granulosa cell and Sertoli-Leydig tumors; SCSTs are 3-7% of ovarian tumors, incidence about 2.1 per million women in Europe.
  • JGCT is described as the most common SCST in children and adolescents, mean age of onset 13 years; adult GCT accounts for up to 95% of GCTs, mean onset 50-55 years.
  • JGCT can manifest with hyperestrogenism and pseudoprecocity, but no studies suggest an association with endometrial pathology (unlike adult GCT, where endometrial hyperplasia occurs in 20-60% and cancer in 1-20%).
  • Lymph node data: in cited series with JGCT (Karalok 10 JGCTs 0/5 positive nodes; Wang 35 JGCTs 0/14; Brown 27 JGCTs, 0/7 in stage I-II and 0/2 in III-IV) no node metastasis was found; lymphadenectomy did not improve disease-free or overall survival and is not recommended by international guidelines for SCST.
  • Cystectomy 'might be well tolerated' in JGCT but a recent review of early-stage JGCT described a 25% recurrence rate with one of four relapsing after 3 months in the same ovary; in that review incomplete surgical staging was significantly associated with disease-free survival and was the only risk factor for recurrence.
  • Laparoscopy seems well tolerated in JGCTs (small series and case reports); surgical staging (peritoneal washing, omentectomy, peritoneal biopsies, no routine lymphadenectomy) should always be performed, and restaging is suggested after incomplete staging.
  • Authors' conclusion: unilateral salpingo-oophorectomy is safer than cystectomy for fertility-sparing surgery in adult GCT, and most evidence comes from retrospective studies and case reports.

Presentation

  • JGCT can manifest with hyperestrogenism and pseudoprecocity

Treatments reported

  • Fertility-sparing surgery (cystectomy vs unilateral salpingo-oophorectomy), staging, laparoscopy (JGCT-specific cited data)
    Outcome: Cystectomy in early JGCT: recurrence rate 25% in a cited review (one of four relapsing at 3 months in the same ovary); incomplete staging the only risk factor for recurrence in that review

Notes

Secondary review; the JGCT figures (25% recurrence after cystectomy, 35-JGCT series) come from a cited review and are not independently reported. Journal, first author and DOI not stated in the fetched text.
