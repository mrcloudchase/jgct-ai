Central precocious puberty and granulosa cell ovarian tumor in an 8-year old female.

Why it matters for JGCT

Unusual JGCT arising within months of documented central precocious puberty with normal ovaries on prior ultrasound; hypothesis-generating single case.

Key findings

  • Caucasian girl with breast development (B2) and pubic hair (PH2) at 7 years 9 months, evaluated at 8 years 5 months: GnRH-stimulated peak LH 6.4 and FSH 13.4 mU/mL, bone age advanced (SDS 2.1), tumor markers negative, pelvic ultrasound with normal ovaries; diagnosed with central precocious puberty.
  • Four months later, before GnRH-analog treatment, she had vaginal bleeding, a palpable pelvic fullness and increased abdominal girth; basal FSH fell to 0.1, estradiol rose to 65 pg/mL, progesterone to 1.7 ng/mL and CA-125 to 74.3 U/mL.
  • Ultrasound showed a 13 x 8 cm left ovarian lesion and MRI a capsulated mixed mass of 13 x 13.7 x 6 cm; laparotomy with left salpingo-oophorectomy achieved complete resection; histology showed sex-stromal cell proliferation with high mitotic activity and low-grade atypia, diagnosed as juvenile granulosa cell tumor.
  • No chemotherapy or radiotherapy was given; at one month estradiol, progesterone and CA-125 had returned to prior values and ultrasound showed no pelvic mass; at 7 months there was no recurrence; MRI of the hypothalamic-pituitary region showed a pineal cyst.
  • The authors propose that FSH overstimulation or aberrant FSH receptor activation may have contributed to tumor growth (FSHR mutations not investigated) and that CPP and granulosa cell tumors might not be coincidentally associated; this is the authors' hypothesis.

Presentation

  • breast enlargement and pubic hair at 7 years 9 months
  • central precocious puberty (GnRH-stimulated LH and FSH elevated)
  • vaginal bleeding 4 months after CPP diagnosis
  • palpable pelvic fullness

Treatments reported

  • Laparotomy, left salpingo-oophorectomy with complete resection; no chemotherapy or radiotherapy (primary; n = 1)
    Outcome: Hormones returned to earlier values at 1 month; no recurrence at 7 months

Notes

Stage, IHC and molecular testing are not reported; diagnosis rests on H&E. Follow-up is only 7 months. Journal and authors not stated in the retrieved text.
