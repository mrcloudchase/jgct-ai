Why it matters for JGCT

German national registry for rare pediatric tumors; JGCT is not named in the registry entry, so its role as a JGCT data source is not stated by this record itself.

Key findings

  • Observational, prospective, case-only patient registry (STEP 2.0) of rare solid tumors in children and adolescents; sponsor University Hospital Tuebingen; status RECRUITING (verified 2024-09); start 2023-01-11; estimated enrollment 10,000; target duration 50 years; primary completion estimated 2050-01.
  • Aim: collect and evaluate experience and data on diagnosis and treatment of rare childhood tumors; includes biological and molecular genetic research and collaboration with the European EXPeRT group.
  • Eligibility: diagnosis of a rare solid tumor from the neonatal period to 18 years; written consent; not recorded in another GPOH (German Society for Pediatric Oncology and Hematology) study or register; young adults may be registered or referred on request with consent.
  • Primary outcome: event-free survival (time from study entry to failure of induction therapy, recurrence or death from any cause), time frame 5 years.
  • Data collected include pathology (reference pathology) findings, name, birth date, gender and consent; pseudonymised data are to be shared with national and international rare-tumor databases.
  • The registry record does not mention juvenile granulosa cell tumor or sex cord-stromal tumors by name and has no posted results.

Notes

Earlier version of this record said the registry was the source of the 113-patient series pmid-40272823; the registry entry read this run does not state that, so the link is not asserted here. No results posted.
