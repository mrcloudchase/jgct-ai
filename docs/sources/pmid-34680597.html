Why it matters for JGCT

Secondary review of omics in rare ovarian cancers; the JGCT content is limited to a Table 2 entry (GSP mutation 30%, AKT1), one sentence on JGCT RNA-Seq, and general granulosa tumor background.

Key findings

  • Narrative review of genomic, transcriptomic, proteomic and metabolomic (omics) studies in rare ovarian cancers; JGCT appears only briefly, as part of the granulosa cell tumor discussion and in Table 2.
  • The review describes granulosa cell tumors as malignant sex cord-stromal tumors constituting about 5% of ovarian malignancies, of two types (adult and juvenile), and that traditional chemotherapy has limited effect in advanced or recurrent disease; GOG264 (NCT01042522, BEP vs carboplatin-paclitaxel) is cited as ongoing.
  • Table 2 lists juvenile granulosa cell tumor genomic alterations as 'GSP mutation (30%), AKT1', contrasting adult granulosa (FOXL2 402C>G missense, >95%, called pathognomonic) and Sertoli-Leydig (DICER1, 90%, including germline). No primary citation or sample size for the JGCT figure is given in the text.
  • Transcriptomics: the review states that a study performing RNA-Seq of juvenile granulosa cell tumors found genes in cytokine/hormone signaling and cell division-related processes differentially regulated (no numbers given).
  • The authors conclude that omics approaches, especially integrated multi-omics, could help define molecular subtypes, biomarkers and therapies for rare ovarian cancers, and that standardization is needed before clinical use.

Genetic and molecular findings

  • GSP (GNAS): mutation (listed as 'GSP mutation' in the review's Table 2) (30% (as stated in review Table 2; no primary source or cohort size given in the text))

  • AKT1: listed as characteristic genomic alteration of juvenile granulosa tumors

Notes

Review without primary patient data; JGCT molecular statements are uncited summaries (GSP/GNAS 30% not traceable within the text). 'GSP mutation' is the review's term. Journal and first author not stated in the retrieved full text.
