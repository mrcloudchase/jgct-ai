Large malignant ovarian tumors during pregnancy: two cases.

Why it matters for JGCT

Case series of two large ovarian tumors in pregnancy, one a JGCT with elevated AFP and CA-125 treated with surgery plus BEP; only that case is JGCT.

Key findings

  • Two cases of large ovarian malignancy in pregnancy at Zhejiang University Women's Hospital are reported; only case 2 was a juvenile granulosa cell tumor (case 1 was a grade I borderline mucinous tumor, 45 cm, and is not JGCT).
  • JGCT patient: 24-year-old at 32 weeks of gestation with a 28x25x12 cm multiloculated cystic right ovarian mass with rich blood flow; AFP 266.8 ng/mL, CA-125 466.9 U/L, CEA 0.4 ng/mL.
  • Lower segment cesarean (live girl, 1,800 g, Apgar 10) with right adnexectomy (30x25x15 cm smooth, unruptured tumor; 2,000 mL cyst fluid aspirated), partial omentectomy, pelvic lymph node dissection and peritoneal biopsies; pelvic fluid cytology negative; frozen section suggested JGCT and final pathology confirmed right ovarian JGCT with fallopian tube and 19 lymph nodes negative.
  • Postoperative AFP fell to 39.5 ng/mL and CA-125 to 359.9 U/mL at day 6; she received three cycles of first-line BEP (from 23 Nov 2012, then 25 Dec 2012 and 23 Jan 2013) with leuprolide, which the authors say is recommended by FIGO for cord stromal tumors; stage is not explicitly stated.
  • Follow-up at 2 years showed she remained healthy.
  • The authors state that stage Ia granulosa cell tumors can be treated conservatively in pregnancy, and that BEP, etoposide-cisplatin and cisplatin-vinblastine-bleomycin protocols show no adverse effects on the fetus (their general statements, not data from the cases).

Presentation

  • huge right cystic pelvic/abdominal mass found on ultrasound at 32 weeks
  • weight gain 18 kg
  • free pelvic fluid

Treatments reported

  • Cesarean section at 32 weeks with fertility-preserving surgery (right adnexectomy, partial omentectomy, pelvic lymph node dissection, peritoneal biopsies), then 3 cycles of BEP (bleomycin, etoposide, cisplatin) plus leuprolide (to preserve ovarian function), starting 10 days after surgery (adjuvant after surgery in pregnancy; n = 1)
    Outcome: healthy at 2-year follow-up

Notes

Stage of the JGCT not stated although pelvic fluid and nodes were negative; the choice of adjuvant BEP is not explained by stage. Case 1 (borderline mucinous) is out of scope.
