Why it matters for JGCT

Trial of first-line chemotherapy for malignant ovarian sex cord-stromal tumors; JGCT is not named as an eligible subtype and the minimum age of 14 means it could include only adolescent patients.

Key findings

  • Multicenter, prospective, randomized phase 3 trial (SCST-01; sponsor/PI Beihua Kong, Shandong University; Qilu Hospital, Jinan, China) comparing paclitaxel plus carboplatin (TC/PT) with BEP in newly diagnosed ovarian malignant sex cord-stromal tumors after surgery; start 2015-04; status RECRUITING (verified 2023-04); estimated enrollment 132.
  • Primary objective: progression-free survival of paclitaxel/carboplatin relative to BEP; secondary: toxicity, overall survival, response rate in measurable disease. Follow-up every 3 months for 2 years, every 6 months for 3 years, then yearly.
  • Eligibility: Chinese women aged 14-65 years (listing says age 65 or younger; minimum age 14), stage IIA-IVB, histologically confirmed granulosa cell, granulosa-theca, Sertoli-Leydig, steroid cell, gynandroblastoma, unclassified SCST or SCTAT; surgery within 8 weeks; Karnofsky score 60 or more.
  • Arms: PT (paclitaxel 175 mg/m2 + carboplatin AUC 5-6, 4-6 courses) vs BEP (bleomycin 30000 IU IM days 1-3, etoposide 100 mg/m2 and cisplatin 20 mg/m2 days 1-5, 3 courses good risk or 4 poor risk).
  • Juvenile granulosa cell tumor is not named; the trial population is mainly adult-type ovarian stromal tumors, with a minimum age of 14. No results posted.

Treatments reported

  • Paclitaxel 175 mg/m2 IV over 3 h plus carboplatin AUC 5-6 IV on day 1, every 21 days, 4-6 courses (arm 1, experimental) (newly diagnosed stage IIA-IVB ovarian malignant sex cord-stromal tumor after initial surgery)
    Outcome: Planned; no results posted
  • Bleomycin 30000 IU IM days 1-3, etoposide 100 mg/m2 IV days 1-5, cisplatin 20 mg/m2 IV days 1-5, every 21 days, 3 courses (good risk) or 4 courses (poor risk) (arm 2, active comparator) (same)
    Outcome: Planned; no results posted

Notes

Registry listing only; no results posted. The brief summary says 'paclitaxel and cisplatin (PT)' while the outline uses paclitaxel and carboplatin; the arms section specifies carboplatin.
