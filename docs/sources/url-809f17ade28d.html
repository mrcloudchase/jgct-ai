Why it matters for JGCT

Second-line paclitaxel data for recurrent ovarian stromal tumors (mostly adult-type); shows low objective response in an adult-dominated population and is not JGCT-specific.

Key findings

  • GOG-0187, single-arm phase II (Gynecologic Oncology Group/NRG Oncology; PI Linda Van Le), start 2000-11-20, completed 2016-07-16; 31 women enrolled and evaluated; conditions listed include adult-type ovarian granulosa cell tumor, gynandroblastoma, Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor, SCTAT, steroid cell tumor.
  • Population: recurrent ovarian stromal tumor with measurable disease (GOG RECIST) after no more than one prior chemotherapy regimen; ages <40 (n=7), 40-49 (n=8), 50-59 (n=9), 60 or more (n=7); 20 White, 7 Black, 4 Asian.
  • Primary outcome, probability of complete clinical response: 3.2% (80% CI 0.74-15.30); one patient had an indeterminate response.
  • Secondary: median progression-free survival 10.0 months (80% CI 6.0-19.6); median overall survival 73.6 months (80% CI 63.7-112.9); 16 of 31 patients died.
  • Adverse events (CTC 2.0/CTCAE 4.0): serious events in 1 patient each for neutrophil count decrease, WBC decrease, lower limb muscle weakness, hyperglycemia, injection site reaction and febrile neutropenia; common non-serious events included neutropenia 21/31, leukopenia 19/31, anemia 20/31, dermatologic 22/31, pain 22/31, neurologic 21/31.
  • Secondary aim of evaluating inhibin for predicting response is stated in the protocol; no inhibin results are posted. The record does not name JGCT; eligible patients were adults.

Treatments reported

  • Paclitaxel IV over 3 h on day 1, every 21 days until progression or unacceptable toxicity (second-line, recurrent ovarian stromal tumor with measurable disease and at most one prior chemotherapy regimen; n = 31)
    Outcome: Complete clinical response 3.2% (80% CI 0.74-15.30); median PFS 10.0 months (80% CI 6.0-19.6); median OS 73.6 months (80% CI 63.7-112.9); 16/31 deaths

Notes

Registry results record only; no peer-reviewed publication read. 'Adult Type Ovarian Granulosa Cell Tumor' is the listed condition; no JGCT patients are identified.
