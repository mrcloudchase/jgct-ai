Why it matters for JGCT

Registered study of DICER1 in pediatric ovarian and testicular sex cord-stromal tumors including JGCT; the record reports design only and no results.

Key findings

  • Children's Oncology Group study ARAR12B2 (sponsor COG; collaborator NCI; PI Dana A. Hill), observational, retrospective, case-only; status COMPLETED (primary completion 2016-07; start 2012-04); estimated enrollment 50.
  • Objective: assess the role of DICER1 mutations and miRNA pathways in the etiology and progression of ovarian or testicular sex cord-stromal tumors.
  • Method: archived fixed and frozen tumor tissue is analyzed for DICER1 mutation and mRNA expression by RT-PCR and laser microdissection, then correlated with age, diagnosis, pathology report, presenting symptoms, family history, treatment, outcome and follow-up.
  • Eligible tissue: from the COG ATBR01 B1 tissue bank and the International Pleuropulmonary Blastoma Registry, Children's Hospital of Boston and Massachusetts General Hospital; ovarian Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor, juvenile granulosa cell tumor, adult granulosa cell tumor, gynandroblastoma, SCTAT, undifferentiated SCST, Sertoli and Leydig tumors; testicular granulosa cell, Sertoli, Leydig, Sertoli-Leydig and undifferentiated stromal tumors.
  • Primary outcome: correlation between DICER1 mutations and miRNA pathways with outcomes using descriptive analysis. No results are posted on the registry record.

Notes

No results posted; do not cite findings from this record.
