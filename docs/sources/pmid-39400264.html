Why it matters for JGCT

Supports DICER1 germline testing in JGCT with anaplasia or high mitotic index and gives context on pelvic surveillance for DICER1 carriers; very few JGCT cases in the cohort (n=2).

Key findings

  • Registry report (International PPB/DICER1 Registry, International OTST Registry, NCI DICER1 study) of 713 participants with germline DICER1 P/LP variant from 38 countries (574 registry plus 139 non-overlapping NCI participants); 61% (437/713) female, median enrollment age 9 years, median follow-up 35 months.
  • JGCT is mentioned only marginally: Figure 2 legend lists 'juvenile granulosa cell tumor (n=2)' among other ovarian tumors in germline DICER1 carriers; the Discussion states that DICER1-related ovarian sarcoma and JGCT have been reported, with SLCT the most common ovarian tumor.
  • Table 2 (testing indications) includes 'Ovarian juvenile granulosa cell tumor (especially if anaplasia and/or high mitotic index)' as an indication to consider germline DICER1 testing.
  • 103 participants had ovarian Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor (SLCT) at median age 14 years (range 11 months-65 years); 13% diagnosed before age 8 and 4% before age 4; 11 had metachronous contralateral SLCT at median 45 months; 15% of all primary ovarian tumors occurred before age 8.
  • Among 87 SLCT patients since the 2018 guidelines, surveillance-detected SLCT (10) were stage IA in 9/10 without chemotherapy, versus 42/77 stage IA and 34/77 chemotherapy among symptomatic cases (90% vs 55% stage IA, p=.032; chemotherapy 10% vs 44%, p=.038); median tumor size 6.6 cm vs 13.0 cm (p=.041).
  • Registry now recommends starting pelvic ultrasound every 6 months at detection of a germline DICER1 variant (previously age 8-10), continuing at least to age 40 with shared decision-making thereafter.
  • Authors note ultrasound is more sensitive than biochemical markers for gynecologic tumor surveillance and that rupture is associated with higher stage and worse prognosis (stated for SLCT).

Genetic and molecular findings

  • DICER1: germline pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants (nonsense 513/713, splice/intronic 87, missense 39, large deletion 23) with tumor RNase IIIb hotspot second hit (713 carriers in registry cohort; 2 had ovarian JGCT)
