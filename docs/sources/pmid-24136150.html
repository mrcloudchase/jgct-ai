DICER1 hotspot mutations in non-epithelial gonadal tumours.

Why it matters for JGCT

Mixed series of gonadal non-epithelial tumours; the only JGCT-specific datum is 0/4 pure ovarian JGCTs DICER1 hotspot-mutated, plus one SLCT with JGCT components that was mutated. Suggests DICER1 RNase IIIb hotspots are not a feature of JGCT, in contrast to SLCT.

Key findings

  • Sanger sequencing of the DICER1 RNase IIIa and IIIb domains in 154 gonadal tumours (135 female, 19 male) and 43 extra-gonadal germ cell tumours found 14 heterozygous RNase IIIb mutations in 14/197 non-epithelial tumours (7.1%).
  • Juvenile granulosa cell tumour: 0/4 ovarian JGCTs carried a DICER1 hotspot mutation (Table 2a).
  • Sex cord-stromal tumours overall were mutated in 9/28 (32%): Sertoli-Leydig cell tumour (SLCT) 8/15 (53%), unclassified SCST 1/7 (14%), Leydig cell tumour 0/1, SCST with annular tubules 0/1.
  • One SLCT with components of JGCT (16-year-old female) carried c.5429A>T, p.D1810V, a variant not previously reported; the table lists it under SLCT, not JGCT.
  • Gonadal germ cell tumours were mutated in 5/118 (4.2%); 0/43 extra-gonadal GCTs, 0/7 small-cell carcinoma of the ovary hypercalcaemic type and 0/1 ovarian embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma were mutated.
  • In all five patients with matched constitutional DNA, the mutations were somatic; mutations clustered at the RNase IIIb metal-binding sites, with most at D1810 and E1813.
  • Median age of mutation-positive patients was 18 years (range 20 months-54 years) versus 17 years (birth-66 years) for mutation-negative (P=0.72).
  • The authors conclude that DICER1 mutation analysis may aid classification of histologically problematic SCSTs; the study could not test germline DNA in all mutation-positive patients and had small numbers in some subtypes.

Genetic and molecular findings

  • DICER1: RNase IIIb hotspot mutations (E1705, D1709, E1788, D1810, E1813); none in RNase IIIa (JGCT 0/4 (ovary); overall 14/197 non-epithelial tumours (7.1%); SLCT 8/15 (53%))
    One mutated tumour (D1810V, 16-year-old female) was an SLCT with components of JGCT; combined with Heravi-Moussavi et al., 34/58 SLCTs (59%) mutated

Notes

Patient overlap is possible with Heravi-Moussavi et al. 2012, which the paper builds on (the 'previous study'); this paper's cohort is described as a follow-up and new samples. Table 3 has alignment problems in the extracted text (several rows share one codon change). The text says 135 female gonadal tumours but Table 2a totals 12/135 and the abstract says 14/154 including testis.
