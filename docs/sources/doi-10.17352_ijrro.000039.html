Juvenile granulosa tumor of the ovary: Report of two cases

Why it matters for JGCT

Two pediatric JGCT cases, both large (25-32 cm) stage I tumors treated by surgery alone, including one restaging after cystectomy.

Key findings

  • Two girls, 8 and 11 years, presenting with abdominopelvic pain and a rapidly enlarging abdominal mass (about 25 x 20 cm and 30 x 20 cm on examination) without signs of hormonal hypersecretion.
  • Case 1: ultrasound and CT showed a multicompartment cystic mass; AFP and beta-hCG normal; surgery found a 25 x 20 cm left ovarian tumor with serous effusion; ovariectomy with normal margins; immunostaining confirmed juvenile granulosa cell tumor; multidisciplinary decision for no chemotherapy or radiotherapy.
  • Case 2: CT showed a 32 x 21 cm semi-solid cystic right laterouterine mass; laparoscopic right cystectomy first, histology showed tumor within margins but a partial resection, so she was re-operated with right adnexectomy, peritoneal biopsies and endometrial curettage; final stage pT1a with no residual tumor, normal endometrium and negative peritoneal biopsies.
  • Histology of case 2: predominantly cystic tumor of partially luteinized proliferating granulosa cells with variable thecal cell proliferation; high vimentin positivity reported.
  • Both patients received adnexectomy without chemotherapy and follow-up (clinical, radiological, and AMH/inhibin in case 2) was uneventful; follow-up duration is not stated.
  • The authors cite 5% of ovarian tumors in children and adolescents, 97% unilateral with mean size 12.5 cm, and 92% 5-year survival, with poor prognostic factors large size, ascites and capsular rupture.

Presentation

  • Case 1 (8 years): 15 days of paroxysmal pelvic pain, abdominal enlargement, weight loss; 25 x 20 cm hard abdominopelvic mass, no hypersecretion signs
  • Case 2 (11 years): 2 months of pelvic pain and abdominal enlargement; 30 x 20 cm mass, no hypersecretion signs

Treatments reported

  • Case 1: left oophorectomy (ovariectomy) with clear margins; no chemotherapy or radiotherapy (primary surgery; n = 1)
    Outcome: Uneventful, clinical/radiological follow-up without particularities
  • Case 2: laparoscopic right cystectomy, then re-operation with right adnexectomy, peritoneal biopsies and endo-uterine curettage; no adjuvant therapy (primary surgery then restaging, pT1a; n = 1)
    Outcome: Follow-up with AMH and inhibin without particularities

Notes

Follow-up duration not given. Case 1 stage not formally stated (no capsular statement). The discussion says the tumor 'measures 12.5 cm on average' (cited) while both cases were 25-32 cm. Immunostaining panel is not detailed beyond vimentin. Full text read from the journal website.
