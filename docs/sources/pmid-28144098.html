Why it matters for JGCT

Case report of JGCT co-occurring with Ollier disease; illustrates that enchondromas can mimic skeletal metastases on staging imaging.

Key findings

  • 2.5-year-old girl presented with precocious puberty (Tanner stage 2) and a hard mass in the right hypochondrium.
  • MRI showed a well-defined right ovarian mass of 10 x 5 x 11 cm with cystic and necrotic areas.
  • MRI also showed multiple small lobulated T2-hyperintense foci in the right femoral neck, trochanters and right iliac blade, initially reported as metastases; radiographs showed expansile lytic lesions with cortical thinning. Bone scan showed no osteoblastic metastasis and bone biopsy showed cartilaginous elements, reported as enchondromas, giving a final diagnosis of Ollier disease with JGCT.
  • Histology: macrofollicles lined by 2-3 layers of granulosa cells with Call-Exner bodies; tumor cells expressed inhibin, calretinin, melan-A (focal) and cytokeratin.
  • Peritoneal aspirate was positive for malignant cells while omental biopsy was not involved; stage was given as 1C3 'as per new FIGO staging'.
  • Treatment was surgical removal followed by chemotherapy per germ cell tumor protocol; breast size and pubic hair regressed a few weeks after surgery. Parents were counseled about chondrosarcoma risk and regular follow-up.
  • The authors state Ollier disease carries an estimated 30%-50% chondrosarcoma risk and argue the association indicates generalized mesodermal dysplasia, so Ollier patients should be screened for mesodermal neoplasms.
  • The authors note maternal ovulation-inducing drug use for 2 years before this conception and propose that estrogen-containing compounds are an etiological factor for granulosa cell tumors.

Presentation

  • precocious puberty (Tanner stage 2)
  • hard mass in right hypochondrium
  • incidental multiple enchondromas on staging MRI mimicking bone metastases

Treatments reported

  • Surgical removal of the ovarian tumor, then chemotherapy 'as per germ cell tumor protocol' (agents and cycles not stated) (adjuvant; n = 1)
    Outcome: Regression of breast size and pubic hair a few weeks after surgery; no longer follow-up reported

Notes

Single case; no molecular testing reported (IDH1/2 or other not discussed). The paper cites mean JGCT presentation age of 13 years from the literature. Chemotherapy regimen is described only as germ cell tumor protocol and outcome follow-up duration is not stated.
