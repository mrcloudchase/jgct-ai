Re-assigning the histologic identities of COV434 and TOV-112D ovarian cancer cell lines.

Why it matters for JGCT

Cell-line identity study: COV434, sometimes used as a JGCT/granulosa cell tumor model, is reassigned to SCCOHT, so experiments using it as a JGCT model should be interpreted accordingly.

Key findings

  • COV434 was originally described as a granulosa cell tumour line from a 26-year-old woman; absence of the adult-type FOXL2 mutation and young age had led others to regard it as consistent with juvenile granulosa cell tumor.
  • Re-review of the original COV434 tumour found diffuse sheets of monotonous small cells with occasional pseudofollicles, clusters of larger rhabdoid-appearing cells and high mitotic activity; the histologic differential was JGCT versus SCCOHT.
  • Original COV434 tumour IHC: negative for SMARCA4 and nuclear SMARCA2, inhibin A, FOXL2, CD10, synaptophysin, chromogranin and CD56; positive for SMARCB1/INI1 and diffuse WT1; focal AE1/AE3; p53 wild-type pattern; mismatch repair intact.
  • Exome and Sanger sequencing found a homozygous SMARCA4 splice-site mutation in both the COV434 primary tumour and the cell line; the line is diploid-like with no copy number alterations.
  • COV434 xenografts in NRG mice (n=6) raised serum calcium from 2.42 +/- 0.11 to 2.87 +/- 0.05 mM (P<0.001), analogous to SCCOHT hypercalcemia; re-expression of SMARCA4 strongly suppressed COV434 proliferation.
  • Authors conclude COV434 is a bona fide SCCOHT cell line rather than a granulosa cell tumour line; TOV-112D (original endometrioid carcinoma) is a dedifferentiated carcinoma line, not SCCOHT.
  • The authors state that COV434 had no further clinical information available beyond the original report; the ovarian cell line OVK18 is also discussed as likely a dedifferentiated carcinoma.

Genetic and molecular findings

  • SMARCA4: splice-site mutation chr19:11145589 G:C, homozygous, in COV434 primary tumour and cell line (1/1)
    dual loss of SMARCA4 and SMARCA2 protein in COV434
  • FOXL2: protein negative by IHC in COV434 original tumour (1/1)
    original report: no FOXL2 hotspot mutation

Conflicts with

  • Chen H 2019: RUNX3 Promotes the Tumorigenic Phenotype in KGN, a Human Granulosa Cell Tumor-Derived Cell Line.

Notes

No JGCT patients; laboratory/pathology re-review. TOV-112D is irrelevant to JGCT. The JGCT-consistent inference about COV434 originates from the cited original report, not from this study's data. Journal and first author not in fetched text.
