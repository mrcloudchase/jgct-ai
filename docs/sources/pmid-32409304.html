Why it matters for JGCT

Minor: gives ASS1 IHC data for 13 JGCT (moderate expression, median histoscore 210), suggesting a possible but untested ADI-PEG20 rationale. JGCT cases are a small comparator group.

Key findings

  • Preclinical proteomics, IHC and xenograft study of rare ovarian cancers; main focus is ASS1 deficiency and arginine-depleting ADI-PEG20 in SCCOHT and clear cell/endometrioid carcinoma, not JGCT.
  • ASS1 IHC on tissue microarrays included juvenile granulosa cell tumor: the Methods text lists n=8 JGCT and the results text says n=8, while Table 1 lists 13 JGCT cases with interpretable ASS1.
  • In sex-cord stromal tumors ASS1 expression was moderate in adult and juvenile GCT (median histoscore 180 for aGCT, n=35; 210 for JGCT).
  • JGCT histoscore distribution (Table 1, n=13): 300 in 2 (15.4%); 200-285 in 5 (38.5%); 100-180 in 3 (23.1%); 10-90 in 0; 0-5 in 3 (23.1%).
  • ASS1 was decreased in Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor (n=45, median 40; 60% below 100) and very low in SCCOHT (absent in 12/31, <5% of cells in 9/31); high in HGSC (178/207 with score 300).
  • ADI-PEG20 inhibited growth of ASS1-deficient cell lines and SCCOHT xenografts (COV434 line, PDX-465); no JGCT models or JGCT treatment data were tested. The authors note they had no survival information for GCT and SLCT cases and that further correlation is needed before ADI-PEG20 could be considered for aggressive GCT.

Genetic and molecular findings

  • ASS1 (protein expression, IHC): moderate expression in JGCT; median histoscore 210 (range 0-300) (JGCT n=13 interpretable: histoscore 300 in 2 (15.4%), 200-285 in 5 (38.5%), 100-180 in 3 (23.1%), 10-90 in 0, 0-5 in 3 (23.1%))
    aGCT n=35 median 180; SLCT n=45 median 40; SCCOHT n=31 median 3.5; HGSC n=207 median 300

Notes

Discrepancy inside the paper: JGCT n=8 in Methods/Results text vs n=13 in Table 1. The COV434 cell line used as an SCCOHT model is a granulosa-like line; the paper treats it as SCCOHT. Journal and first author not in retrieved text.
