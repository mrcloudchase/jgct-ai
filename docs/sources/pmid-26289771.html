Ovarian tumors related to intronic mutations in DICER1: a report from the international ovarian and testicular stromal tumor registry.

Why it matters for JGCT

JGCT appears only as an authors' statement that germline DICER1 testing should be considered in JGCT and other ovarian stromal tumors with DICER1-related personal or family history; no JGCT patient or JGCT sequencing result is reported.

Key findings

  • Two young women with ovarian Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor (SLCT) carried the same novel germline DICER1 intronic variant c.5096-12G>A; no patient in the report had a juvenile granulosa cell tumor.
  • Patient 1: undifferentiated ovarian sarcoma at age 10 (inhibin negative; lacked JGCT features; treated with vincristine, actinomycin D, cyclophosphamide), then FIGO IIA SLCT in the other ovary 4 years later (6 cycles cisplatin, etoposide, bleomycin); alive and well 15 years after the first and 12 years after the second diagnosis.
  • Patient 2: adolescent with SLCT plus lung cyst and thyroid nodule; alive and well more than 1 year after diagnosis.
  • Each ovarian tumor carried the germline variant plus a tumor-specific somatic RNase IIIb mutation (two-hit pattern); TP53 mutated only in the sarcoma.
  • Preliminary OTST Registry data cited: 20 individuals with ovarian SLCT tested positive for germline DICER1 mutations, 5 with splice-site base substitutions (these 2 plus 3 canonical splice-site); earlier registry data cited germline DICER1 mutations in 48% of SLCT.
  • Authors state that DICER1 mutations remain a risk for metachronous ovarian tumors beyond the usual 2-5 year surveillance window and recommend surveillance imaging (usually ultrasound) and genetic counseling.
  • Authors conclude germline DICER1 mutations should be suspected in patients with SLCT, and also in patients with juvenile granulosa cell tumor, gynandroblastoma or any ovarian stromal tumor or sarcoma with a personal or family history of nodular thyroid disease or other DICER1 conditions; somatic tumor testing is the most sensitive way to detect DICER1 mutations.

Presentation

  • Patient 1: dysuria and pelvic pressure (undifferentiated ovarian sarcoma, age 10); amenorrhea and raised testosterone 4 years later (SLCT)

Genetic and molecular findings

  • DICER1: germline intronic c.5096-12G>A (NM_177438.2), duplicating the six-base splice site at the intron 23/exon 24 junction; predicted 10-base intron inclusion, frameshift p.Asp1699AlaFS*8 disrupting RNase IIIb (2 patients (both Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor))
    Not found in SNP databases, 1000 Genomes, other published series or other OTST/PPB Registry samples
  • DICER1: somatic RNase IIIb hotspot missense in each ovarian tumor (p.Asp1709Asn, p.Glu1813Asp, p.Asp1709Val) (all tumors sequenced)
    Patient 1 sarcoma also had TP53 p.Val274Asp

Notes

Study patients had SLCT and an undifferentiated sarcoma, not JGCT (n_jgct_patients 0). The JGCT recommendation is an expert statement in the Conclusion, not derived from JGCT data in this report.
