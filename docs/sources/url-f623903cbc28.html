Why it matters for JGCT

Registry of DICER1-related tumors and carriers; it enrolls sex cord-stromal tumors (Sertoli-Leydig, gynandroblastoma) but does not name JGCT, so relevance to JGCT is indirect (DICER1 context).

Key findings

  • International PPB/DICER1 Registry (Children's Hospitals and Clinics of Minnesota; PI Kris Ann Schultz), observational cohort patient registry; status RECRUITING (verified 2026-10); start 2016-12-06; estimated enrollment 3,400; primary completion estimated 2031-12-06.
  • Inclusion: known or suspected PPB or related thoracic tumor; known or suspected sex cord-stromal tumor including Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor and gynandroblastoma (males or females); other DICER1-related conditions; and individuals with known or suspected DICER1 pathogenic variants regardless of disease.
  • Listed DICER1-associated conditions include Sertoli-Leydig cell tumor, gynandroblastoma, ovarian sarcoma, PPB, lung and renal cysts, cystic nephroma, Wilms tumor, CNS sarcoma, pineoblastoma, pituitary blastoma, multinodular goiter, thyroid carcinoma, nasal chondromesenchymal hamartoma, ciliary body medulloepithelioma, intestinal polyps and embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma. Juvenile granulosa cell tumor is not named in the summary or eligibility text.
  • The registry estimates 30-50 cases of Type I and Ir PPB and 40-60 cases of Type II and III PPB diagnosed annually worldwide.
  • Treatment described is the treating institution's choice: Type I PPB surgery with optional 22-week VAC/VA chemotherapy; Type II/III PPB surgery, chemotherapy (often 36-week IVADo) and sometimes radiation.
  • Primary outcome: event-free survival (7 years); secondary: response, overall survival, quality of life, cardiac and pulmonary outcomes, and incidence of neoplasms in individuals with germline DICER1 variants. Tumor tissue, blood, urine and CSF may be banked. No results posted.
